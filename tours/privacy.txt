LI: Who we are
P: This website, tours.cigalacycling.com, is run by Cigala Cycling Tours Ltd, a company registered in Ireland (company number 780307) ("Cigala Cycling Tours", "we", "us"). We are the controller of the personal data described in this policy.
For any privacy question or request, contact us at tours@cigalacycling.com or +353 40478107.
Our other websites (cigalacycling.com, coaching.cigalacycling.com and retail.cigalacycling.com) each have their own privacy policy covering the services and tools they use. By using this website you confirm that you have read this policy.
LI: What we collect and why
P: Contact and custom tour forms: your name, email address, phone number (custom tour form only) and message. We use them only to answer your enquiry and, for custom tours, to prepare your proposal. Legal basis: your request and our legitimate interest in replying to it.
Bookings: when you book a tour, our booking platform WeTravel collects your name, contact details, the tour you booked and your payment. We use these details to confirm and run your tour, take deposits and balances, and send you the trip information. Legal basis: our contract with you.
Tour details: before the tour we may ask for details such as your bike size and preferences, room sharing, dietary needs, flight times and an emergency contact, and we send you our Waiver of Liability Form to complete. We use this information only to organise your tour and look after you on it. Legal basis: our contract with you and, for the emergency contact, our legitimate interest in your safety.
Health information: if you choose to tell us about a medical condition or allergy, we use it only to keep you safe on the tour and share it only where needed, for example with a hotel or in an emergency. Legal basis: your explicit consent, or protecting your vital interests in an emergency.
Live chat: the messages and any details you choose to share in the chat. We use them to answer you. Legal basis: your request and our legitimate interest in replying to it.
Website analytics and advertising: information about how you use the site, such as pages visited, device, browser and approximate location, collected only if you accept cookies (see "Cookies" below). Legal basis: your consent.
Technical data: like every website, our host's servers automatically record basic technical information (such as IP address, browser and the time of your visit) to keep the site running and secure. Legal basis: our legitimate interest in protecting the website.
Photos: we often take photos and videos on our tours and may share them on our website and social media, as set out in the Waiver of Liability Form. If you would rather not appear, tell us and we will respect it.
We do not store or maintain credit card details, nor do we share customer financial details with any third parties. Card payments are handled by WeTravel and its payment providers.
LI: Cookies
P: When you first visit, a banner asks whether you accept cookies. Until you choose, and if you reject, we do not set analytics or advertising cookies.
If you accept, we use:
- Google Analytics, provided by Google Ireland Limited, to understand how visitors use the site. Google Consent Mode keeps it in a cookie-free state until you accept.
- Meta Pixel, provided by Meta Platforms Ireland Limited, to measure and improve our Facebook and Instagram advertising.
- The OpenWidget live chat, provided by Text S.A. (Poland), which loads automatically after you accept. If you have not accepted, the chat only loads when you click "Chat with us".
The WeTravel booking window, which opens when you click "Book now", may set its own cookies needed to complete your booking.
We also store your cookie choice in your browser (local storage), so we don't ask you again.
You can change your choice at any time with the "Cookie settings" link at the bottom of every page, or by deleting cookies in your browser.
LI: Who receives your data
P: We only share personal data with trusted service providers and partners that help us run the website and your tour, and only as far as they need it:
- WeTravel, our booking and payment platform (WeTravel Inc., United States).
- Hotels, bike rental shops, transfer and transport companies and restaurants that provide services on your tour, which receive the details they need, such as your name, room, bike size, dietary needs and arrival times.
- Event organisers, such as gran fondo and sportive organisers, when we register you for an event included in your tour.
- Hostinger, our website host (EU).
- Google Ireland Limited (Google Analytics, and Google Fonts, which delivers the website's fonts and receives your IP address when a page loads).
- Meta Platforms Ireland Limited (Meta Pixel, only if you accept cookies).
- Text S.A. (OpenWidget live chat).
These providers act on our instructions or under their own privacy terms, and must protect your data. We may also disclose personal data where the law requires it, for example to a court or public authority, or to protect our rights, our customers or others.
Some of these providers may transfer data outside the European Economic Area, for example to the United States. Where they do, they use legal safeguards approved by the European Commission, such as the EU-US Data Privacy Framework or Standard Contractual Clauses. Tour partners outside the EEA receive only what they need to provide your service.
We never sell your personal data.
LI: How long we keep it
P: Contact form and chat enquiries: for as long as needed to deal with your enquiry, and no longer than two years afterwards, unless we need to keep them longer for legal, tax or insurance reasons or to deal with a claim.
Bookings: for as long as Irish tax and company law requires us to keep business records, and as long as needed to deal with any claim.
Tour details and health information: deleted after the tour, unless we need them for an insurance claim or an incident.
Analytics data: kept in Google Analytics in line with our retention settings, after which it is deleted automatically.
LI: How we protect your data
P: The website uses an encrypted connection (HTTPS). Access to our website, email and booking accounts is restricted and password-protected, and we only work with reputable providers that apply their own security measures.
No method of sending or storing data over the internet is completely secure. We take reasonable steps to protect your data, but we cannot guarantee absolute security, and you share information with us at your own risk. If a data breach is likely to affect your rights, we will inform you and the Data Protection Commission as the law requires.
LI: Children
P: This website is not intended for children under 16. We do not knowingly collect personal data from children under 16. Tours for younger riders are booked by a parent or guardian, who provides the rider's details. If you believe a child has sent us personal data, contact us and we will delete it.
LI: Other websites and social media
P: This website links to other websites and platforms, including our other Cigala Cycling sites, WeTravel, WhatsApp, Instagram, Facebook, YouTube, TikTok, X and Threads. When you follow those links or contact us on those platforms, their own privacy policies apply. We are not responsible for the content or privacy practices of websites and platforms we do not control.
LI: Your rights
P: Under the GDPR you have the right to:
- access the personal data we hold about you;
- have it corrected or deleted;
- object to or restrict how we use it;
- receive it in a portable format;
- withdraw your consent at any time (for example through "Cookie settings"). This does not affect anything we did before you withdrew it.
To use any of these rights, email tours@cigalacycling.com. We may need to confirm your identity first. We will reply within one month. We do not make decisions about you based solely on automated processing.
If you are not happy with how we handle your data, please contact us first so we can try to resolve it. You also have the right to complain to the Data Protection Commission in Ireland (www.dataprotection.ie).
