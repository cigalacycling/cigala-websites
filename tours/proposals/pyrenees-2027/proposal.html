The Pyrenees, cols of legend

This itinerary focuses entirely on the French Pyrenees, combining some of the most iconic Tour de France climbs with spectacular mountain roads and three different accommodation bases.

The route is designed to explore different areas of the Pyrenees while incorporating point-to-point riding days, allowing us to travel between locations by bike rather than relying on vehicle transfers. Every day is fully supported by our guides and support vehicle, with easier options always available.

Itinerary

Day by day

Routes are finalised with the group. Full GPX files, distances and elevation are sent before the trip.

DAY1Sat 19 June
Arrival in Luchon & shake-out ride

Welcome to the Pyrenees! Transfer from the airport to Bagnères-de-Luchon, the “Queen of the Pyrenees”. Bike set-up and fitting with our mechanics, then an easy shake-out ride in the valley to loosen the legs after the journey. Welcome briefing and first evening together.

Hotel: Alti Hotel ★★★ · Bagnères-de-Luchon
DAY2Sun 20 June
Col de Portet d’Aspet & Col de Menté
▲ Col de Portet d’Aspet▲ Col de Menté

A loop from Luchon over two climbs full of Tour de France history: the steep Col de Portet d’Aspet and the Col de Menté, through quiet forests and remote mountain villages.

Hotel: Alti Hotel ★★★ · Bagnères-de-Luchon
DAY3Mon 21 June
Peyresourde & Col d’Azet to Saint-Lary
▲ Col de Peyresourde▲ Col d’Azet

Our first point-to-point stage, from base to base by bike. We leave Luchon on the Col de Peyresourde, a Tour de France classic, ride through the Val Louron and over the Col d’Azet, then descend into Saint-Lary-Soulan. Luggage travels with the support vehicle.

Hotel: Mercure Saint-Lary ★★★★ · Saint-Lary-Soulan
DAY4Tue 22 June
Col d’Aspin & Payolle loop
▲ Col d’Aspin▲ Payolle (Hourquette d’Ancizan)

From Saint-Lary we climb the Col d’Aspin. On the way down towards Payolle and the Tourmalet we turn left over the Hourquette d’Ancizan, a quiet high road with wide views, and come back down into the Saint-Lary valley.

Hotel: Mercure Saint-Lary ★★★★ · Saint-Lary-Soulan

DAY5Wed 23 June
Col du Portet & Lac de Cap de Long
▲ Col du Portet▲ Lac de Cap de Long (option)

The queen climb of this side of the range: the Col du Portet (2,215 m), one of the highest roads in the Pyrenees and a modern Tour de France summit finish. Riders who want more can add the climb to the Lac de Cap de Long dam. In the afternoon we transfer with the bikes to Lourdes.

Hotel: Hotel Gallia ★★★★ · Lourdes
DAY6Thu 24 June
Col du Tourmalet
▲ Col du Tourmalet

The legendary Col du Tourmalet (2,115 m), the most climbed pass in Tour de France history. A day to enjoy the climb, the summit and the long descent back down the valley.

Hotel: Hotel Gallia ★★★★ · Lourdes
DAY7Fri 25 June
Col d’Aubisque & Hautacam
▲ Col d’Aubisque▲ Hautacam

The grand finale: up the Val d’Azun and along the famous balcony road to the Col d’Aubisque. Riders who still have legs finish on the slopes of Hautacam. Easier options are always available. Celebration dinner in the evening.

Hotel: Hotel Gallia ★★★★ · Lourdes
DAY8Sat 26 June
Departure day

After breakfast, transfer to the airport. See you next time!
