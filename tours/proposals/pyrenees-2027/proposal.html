The Pyrenees, cols of legend

The Pyrenees are home to some of the most famous climbs in cycling: the Tourmalet, the Aubisque, the Peyresourde and the Col du Portet. We have designed a week that moves across the heart of the range, staying at three bases so every day brings new roads, quieter valleys and authentic mountain villages.

We start in Luchon, the “Queen of the Pyrenees”, cross to Saint-Lary-Soulan for the Col du Portet, and finish in Lourdes after riding the Tourmalet, the Aubisque and Hautacam. Every day is fully supported by our guides and support vehicle, with easier options always available.

Itinerary

Day by day

Routes, distances and climbing are indicative and will be finalised with the group. GPX files are sent before the trip.

DAY1Sat 19 June
Arrival in Luchon & shake-out ride
30 km+400 m

Welcome to the Pyrenees! Transfer from the airport to Bagnères-de-Luchon, the “Queen of the Pyrenees”. Bike set-up and fitting with our mechanics, then an easy shake-out ride along the valley to loosen the legs after the journey. Welcome briefing and first evening together.

Hotel: Alti Hotel, Luchon
DAY2Sun 20 June
Superbagnères & Col du Portillon
75 km+2,100 m

Straight from the hotel we climb to Superbagnères, a former Tour de France summit finish with huge views over the Luchon valley. In the afternoon we cross the Col du Portillon into Spain and return through the Val d’Aran.

Hotel: Alti Hotel, Luchon
DAY3Mon 21 June
Col de Peyresourde & Col d’Azet to Saint-Lary
65 km+1,900 m

Our first point-to-point stage. We leave Luchon on the Col de Peyresourde, a Tour de France classic, then ride through the Val Louron and over the Col d’Azet before descending into Saint-Lary-Soulan. Luggage travels with the support vehicle.

Hotel: Mercure, Saint-Lary-Soulan
DAY4Tue 22 June
Col du Portet
55 km+1,700 m

A day for the queen climb of this side of the range: the Col du Portet (2,215 m), one of the highest roads in the Pyrenees and a modern Tour de France summit finish. Long, steady and spectacular above the Néouvielle massif. Afternoon to rest and recover.

Hotel: Mercure, Saint-Lary-Soulan

DAY5Wed 23 June
Col d’Aspin & Col du Tourmalet to Lourdes
115 km+2,600 m

The big day. We climb the Col d’Aspin, then the legendary Col du Tourmalet (2,115 m), the most climbed pass in Tour de France history. A long descent through Luz-Saint-Sauveur and the Gave de Pau valley brings us to Lourdes.

Hotel: Hotel Gallia, Lourdes
DAY6Thu 24 June
Col du Soulor & Col d’Aubisque
105 km+2,200 m

From Lourdes we ride up the Val d’Azun to the Col du Soulor and on along the famous balcony road to the Col d’Aubisque, with its giant bikes at the summit. Return the same way for one of the most scenic descents of the week.

Hotel: Hotel Gallia, Lourdes
DAY7Fri 25 June
Hautacam (or Luz Ardiden)
60 km+1,600 m

The grand finale on the slopes of Hautacam, scene of many Tour de France battles. Riders who want more can choose Luz Ardiden instead; an easier valley option is always available. Celebration dinner in the evening.

Hotel: Hotel Gallia, Lourdes
DAY8Sat 26 June
Departure day

After breakfast, transfer to the airport. See you next time!
