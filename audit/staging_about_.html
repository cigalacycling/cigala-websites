About Cigala Cycling

Our mission

Since its founding in 2016, Cigala Cycling’s mission is to bring all of the cyclist’s needs in one location and make them easily available at the consumer’s fingertips.

Whether it is to help you improve your cycling & triathlon abilities, to help you shop the right product or to provide you with the most unforgettable bike tour, Cigala Cycling has you covered.

Coaching

Cycling & Triathlon Coaching

Coaching is our CORE.
#TrainSmarterRideFaster is our vision.

Cigala Cycling was founded by the Italian head coach Matteo Cigala when he moved to Ireland.

A former pro cyclist with 28 years in the sport, Matteo combines sports science, racing experience and close links with professional teams to help riders of every level train smarter and ride faster.

Cigala Cycling offers the highest level of cycling coaching and triathlon coaching.

Individual coaching packages, sportive coaching packages and group coaching packages available.

To date, Matteo has coached more than 1,000 athletes all over the world.

Bike tours

Tours

Memorable Adventures. New Friends. Shared Passion. Lifetime Experience.

Cigala Cycling Tours provides fully supported road and gravel tours across Europe’s best cycling roads, in small groups led by former pros.

For over a decade we have guided more than 700 cyclists on our tours.

We ride in more than 20 destinations across Europe, from the Dolomites, the Alps and Tuscany to Sicily, Sardinia and Puglia, from the Pyrenees and Provence to the Algarve, and on to Slovenia, Croatia and Cyprus.

Cigala Cycling Tours is the Official Tour Operator of the Maratona dles Dolomites, offers official event entry packages for the Gran Fondo Strade Bianche & Gran Fondo Il Lombardia, and is an Official Event Partner of the Algarve Granfondo.
