Ever wondered what a ride built entirely around the road, the people, and the moment would feel like? I did too.

 

Cigala Cycling Tours grew out of years of coaching riders of every level — and the realization that people don’t just want to ride, they want to experience. So I built tours around exactly that: hand-picked roads, small groups, no crowds, no shortcuts.

Every trip is guided by a team of former pros who know these roads because they’ve ridden them for years, not read them off a script. Good food, real local stops, and the kind of ride you actually remember.

More than a decade in, that idea hasn’t changed.

We’d love to welcome you on the road.

– Matteo Cigala, Tours Director –

Meet Our Tours Director

Ride With Matteo Cigala

Matteo Cigala is a former Gran Fondo European Champion, Vice Gran Fondo World Champion and National Gravel Champion. Founder & Tours Director of Cigala Cycling Tours & Founders & Head Coach at Cigala Cycling Coaching.

Every tour is carefully designed by Matteo and let either by him or a member of the team — not a seasonal tour guide — so you’re riding with people who know these roads from racing on them and organising tours for over a decade.

Good to Know

Frequently Asked Questions

What fitness level do I need?

Our tours are graded by difficulty (beginner, advanced & expert), from relaxed coastal routes to high-mountain climbs, so riders of allfitness levels can find a suitable trip.

Do you offer bike rental?

Yes, road, gravel, mtb, or e-bikes rental are available for every destination.

How do airport transfers work?

Airport transfers are arranged at a set time for all public tours — you will find the details in the description. We can also arrange a private transfer at a supplement if you arrive at a different time. Just let know your flight details when booking.

Are support vehicles included?

Every guided tour includes a support vehicle, mechanical assistance, water & snacks throughout each day’s ride.
