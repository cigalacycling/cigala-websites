H1: OUR STORY
P: Cigala Cycling Tours was born from a simple idea: to share the joy of **discovering the world by bike**, guided by real experience, passion, and the highest level of professionalism.
P: Our founder, **Matteo Cigala**, spent years racing at professional and elite level across Europe. As very successful coach, he works with riders of all abilities — from beginners building their first structured training plan to pros riding the biggest races in the world.
P: Through his coaching business, he learned something valuable: people don’t just want to ride. They want to **experience**. They want **connection**, **challenge**, **culture**, and a **sense of achievement**.
P: After years spent leading training camps, Matteo realised something **powerful**: the best rides were never about the distance or the speed —they were about the **moments**. The quiet beauty of a **road chosen with intention**. The **conversation **that carries you up a climb. The beautiful **coffee stop** and the chosen **local restaurants**. The **pride and joy** that blooms at a summit earned together.
P: He wanted to bring that feeling to **every cyclist**. So he created tours shaped with purpose and crafted with care—no generic routes, no crowded groups, no touristy shortcuts. Just **authentic**, **deeply personal adventures** designed for riders who crave **connection**, meaning, and the kind of **experiences **that stay with you long after the journey ends.
P: What began as a passion project quickly grew into a dedicated **premium tour company.** Drawing on his racing background, local knowledge, and coaching expertise, Matteo crafted tours that balance performance and pleasure: challenging but achievable, supported yet adventurous, social yet personal.
P: Today, after **more than 10 years running tours**, Cigala Cycling Tours welcomes riders from** around the world** to explore Europe’s most iconic and hidden cycling destinations.
P: **Each tour reflects Matteo’s standards **— small groups, handcrafted routes, professional atmosphere, exceptional organisation, and the belief that cycling is one of the best ways to experience a place.
P: Our **mission **is simple: create** memorable adventures** while meeting **new friends**, sharing the **same passion** that leaves you a **lifetime experience**.
P: Our story is still being written, **one climb, one ride, and one unforgettable journey at a time.**
