H2: FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS
H2: What would the most suitable package for me?
P: The Advanced package is for those who knows ahead of time that they are committed to a disciplined training program and wants to see definitive seasonal goals achieved. The Advanced is our most popular package and covers everything that you need.
P: The Premium package is for those triathletes that want very best in life and the utmost from their personal coach.
Those choosing the Premium can be anyone, from first-timer to seasoned pro.
H2: Do you only coach people who compete?
P: No. We coach all sorts of people. You may be interested in losing weight, trying to complete a 100 mile ride, being fitter, completing a sprint event or and Ironman event.
H2: What is Training Peaks?
P: Training Peaks is the leading online training logs in the market. It allows you to follow your plan in a interactive way with your coach.
It enables planning and analysing your training in the same manner as top professional athletes would do.
P: Your coach will be able to make your plan and review your session via the platform. You can leave comments for the coach and get access to many graphs and analytics tools.
P: You can also have your plan/calendar and all of the other features via the mobile app and have all of your training stored in your pocket.
With TP, you can also sync your upcoming training sessions and completed rides from your Garmin cycle computer, Zwift, Wahoo ELEMNT or Android device.
H2: How many hours do I need to follow the plan?
P: It depends by your training availability.
We hand-build your plan only for you and to suits your needs.
Your life commitments are important and we design the plan around them.
If you have 5 hours a week available to train, we will schedule the training for 5 hours.
If you have 10 hours, we will schedule 10 hours and so on.
H2: What age do need to be to be coached?
P: We coach all ages. From 14 years old to….wait there is no limit. One of our past coached riders was 75 years old.
We have a 20% student discount for all people below U23
