P: Matteo Cigala is a cycling coach based in Wicklow, Ireland. With 28 years of experience in cycling, over a decade of coaching, hundreds of race victories, and formal qualifications including a Business Management Degree (BA) and a Diploma in Science and Sports, Matteo has built a reputation as one of the leading cycling coaches in Ireland.
P: In 2015, he brought his vision to life: connecting Ireland to the Italian cycling scene and delivering some of the most advanced, up-to-date coaching methods.
P: Through continuous collaboration with professional cyclists, WorldTour teams, top-level sport sciences and coaches, he ensures that his athletes benefit from the same performance standards used at the highest level of the sport.
P: Today, Matteo is widely regarded as the most successful cycling coach in Ireland of the last decade, coaching athletes of all levels — from beginners to competitive club riders to WorldTour professionals.

H3: THE IRISH CHAPTER — DOMINANCE, CONSISTENCY & LEADERSHIP
P: After moving to Ireland, Matteo became one of the nation’s most successful cyclists.
P: During the Rás Tailteann (UCI 2.2), Matteo finished as Top County Rider 20 times across five editions (2016–2024), including six podiums — a record unmatched by any other rider.
P: In 2017, he finished the season as Ireland’s highest-ranking Elite domestic rider, winning the Cycling Ireland A1 Classification.
P: In 2018, Matteo raced professionally with the Holdsworth Pro Racing Team, competing in major international events including the Tour of Qinghai Lake (UCI 2.HC) — China’s biggest stage race
P: That same period brought a historic achievement: alongside his athlete Greg Swinand, Matteo broke the Irish Hour Record twice — first in Colorado, then in Aguascalientes, Mexico with a distance of 48.469 km.
P: ​From 2019 to 2020, Matteo served as Sports Director and Head of Athlete Performance at Ireland’s only UCI professional team, EvoPro Racing, further expanding his expertise in elite performance management.
