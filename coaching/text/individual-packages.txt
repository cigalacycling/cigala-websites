H2: FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS
H3: What would the most suitable package for me?
P: It depends on your needs: budget, tools available and how much feedback you want to get. The main difference between packages is the level of feedback and communication with the coach. If you are a person that is able to follow the training without the human aspect of a coach, then the Basic will be enough. However, the Advanced Package is the most popular because not only you have access to Matteo as you coach with feedback, phone calls, training analysis, but because you get Vekta membership included worth €19.99/month.
H3: Do you only coach people who race?
P: No. We coach all sorts of people. You may be interested in losing weight, trying to complete a 100 mile ride, being fitter, completing a sportive, taking part in a triathlon, or bike-racing. All that we ask is you ride a bike, and want to improve in some way.
H3: What is Vekta?
P: [Vekta ](https://joinvekta.com/)is one of the leading online training platforms, giving you the same tools professional riders use. Follow your plan interactively, track every session, and communicate directly with your coach. Access advanced metrics like Critical Power, W′, and beyond-FTP insights, adaptive training zones, and detailed performance analytics — all from your computer or mobile app. Sync your rides from Garmin, Zwift, Wahoo, or Android, and make every ride count toward your goals.
H3: I am used to Training Peaks, do I need to make the switch to Vekta or can we work also on Training Peaks?
P: You don’t need to switch if you’re already comfortable with TrainingPeaks — we can absolutely continue working there. That said, Vekta offers some additional features that can give you deeper insights, like Critical Power and W′ metrics, adaptive training zones, and advanced analytics, all presented in a clear, interactive way. So if you’re curious, we can explore Vekta together alongside your current setup — but the most important thing is that your training stays consistent and effective, no matter the platform.
H3: Where the 1to1 sessions take place?
P: The 1to1 sssions take place in Wicklow Town, Co.Wicklow.
H3: How many hours do I need to follow the plan?
P: It depends by your training availability.
We hand-build your plan only for you and to suits your needs. Your life commitments are important and we design the plan around them. If you have 5 hours a week available to train, we will schedule the training for 5 hours. If you have 10 hours, we will schedule 10 hours and so on.
H3: What age do need to be to be coached?
P: We coach all ages. From 14 years old to….wait there is no limit. One of our past coached riders was 75 years old.
We have a 20% student discount for all people below U23.
H3: My event is not until 16 weeks from now, can I still sign up now to the Basic Package?
P: Yes, of course. You will receive everything that you need when you sign up. The plan should be followed for the 12 weeks before the event. Therefore, even if you sign up a bit earlier, you can still take advantage of the nutritional guide, core and stretching exercises, training and lifestyle analysis, nutritional and time management assessment.
