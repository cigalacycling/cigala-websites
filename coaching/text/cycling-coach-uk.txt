H2: FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS
TXT: Which package is right for me?
P: It depends on your budget and how much direct feedback you want. If you’re happy following a plan independently, Basic is enough. Advanced is our most popular package because it includes weekly access to Matteo plus Vekta membership.
TXT: I'm based in the UK — does coaching work remotely?
P: Yes, entirely. Training plans, feedback, video calls and testing are all delivered online. The UK’s time zone alignment with Ireland makes live communication straightforward.
TXT: Do you only coach people who race?
P: No. We coach riders working toward weight loss, their first 100-mile ride, general fitness, sportives, triathlon or racing — the only requirement is that you ride a bike and want to improve.
TXT: Do I need to switch from Training Peaks to Vekta?
P: No — we can continue working in Training Peaks if you’re already comfortable there, or explore Vekta’s Critical Power and W′ analytics alongside it.
TXT: What age do I need to be?
P: We coach all ages, 14 and up — no upper limit. Riders under U23 receive a 20% student discount.
TXT: My event is months away — can I still sign up now?
P: Yes. Based on how far away is the event you can choose any suitable package.
