P: Over & Under. A great session for indoor training.
P: These Over and Under intervals are a lactate threshold workout with surges or accelerations to simulate the racing demands of taking a turn at the front or being in a breakaway. As an indoor workout, it’s very engaging as you keep changing the intensities. Recommendation for this type of session:
LI: Ensure you have tested your for FTP/FTHR recently in order to have accurate training zones.
LI: The shift in intensity is subtle, don’t jump or accelerate too quickly.
LI: Keep your pedal stroke smooth and at a comfortable cadence for you to hit the numbers.

H2: WARM-UP
P: 10 minutes increasing from Zone 1 to Zone 3 (50 to 85% of our FTP/FTHR)
H2: VO₂
P: 1 minute at Zone 5 to activate (120% of FTP/FTHR)
H2: ZONE 2
P: 5 minutes (70% of our FTP/FTHR)
H2: OVER & UNDER
P: 3 sets of 10 minutes alternating 30 seconds at 110% of FTP/FTHR followed by 30 seconds at 90% of FTP/FTHR, 5 minutes recovery between sets.
H2: COOL DOWN
P: 10 minutes (50% of our FTP/FTHR)
