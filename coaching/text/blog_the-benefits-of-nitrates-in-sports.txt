P: In recent years, nitrates in sports, and especially in the cycling performance has become increasingly important and popular.
Recent studies show that supplementation with nitrate (NO3−) can improve performance and cardiovascular (CV) responses, due to an increased nitric oxide (NO) availability.
P: Nitrates are found especially in green leafy vegetables, such as spinach, rocket, chard, arugula, lettuce, celery and root vegetables such as beetroot.
Those are absorbed in the intestines, are then concentrated at least 10 times in the saliva, and in the mouth are transformed into nitrites by bacteria that are located on the tongue.
Nitrites are then partially converted to Nitric Oxide (NO) which, as mentioned, is the main responsible for the effects of nitrate on metabolic functions.
P: In sports performance and especially in cycling, those studies, indicate that a correct intake of nitrates can reduce the cost of oxygen from 10 to 9 ml/min/watt.
This can lead to an improvement of 5/6% in the power generated in a time trial in a group of mid-level cyclists. This improvement is equal to 3 seconds per km in a TT of 30 minutes!
Nitric oxide (NO), also, enhances the efficiency of muscle contraction through a reduction in the cost of the actin-myosin interaction and an improvement in the efficiency of mitochondrial respiration.
