H3: WHY A CYCLING COACH?
P: You know how to ride a bike, right? So why would you need a coach? Many people wouldn’t consider playing tennis or golf without lessons, but because we grew up riding bikes, we assume we can do it all on our own. The truth is, cycling at any competitive level is complex. It’s more than just riding fast — proper fitness, skills, and strategy are essential. You can spend a fortune on the lightest, most aerodynamic bike, but without the right training, it won’t make you faster — though your bike will look amazing!
H3: OUR COACHING APPROACH
P: At Cigala Cycling, we start by understanding your goals, abilities, and limitations.
P: From there, we build an annual training plan that is periodised and tailored with daily sessions designed specifically for you.
P: Your plan is created to maximise your time on the bike and fit around your schedule, goals, and lifestyle.
P: Every plan is hand-crafted, completely customised, and built for your training availability — no two plans are alike, no copy-paste templates.
P: Coaching is a two-way process.
P: With your ongoing feedback, we adjust your training load and schedule as needed to ensure continuous improvement.
P: The result? You train smarter, ride faster, and achieve your goals. That’s our vision.
