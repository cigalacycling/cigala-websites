I am delighted to share my testimonial for my cycling coach Matteo from Cigala Cycling.

After setbacks from a crash, he never pushed me beyond my limits but instead adjusted the training plan as appropriate.

His endearing and infectious personality makes every session enjoyable, creating a positive and supportive environment.

In conclusion, I can confidently say that my decision to work with Matteo from Cigala Cycling has been the catalyst for my growth and success as a cyclist.
His confidence in my abilities has made me a more confident rider. With his expertise, support, and personalized approach, I have achieved my goal of qualifying for the UCI Granfondo World Championship.

I am grateful to have him as my coach and highly recommend him to anyone looking to take their cycling to the next level. With Matteo’s guidance you can achieve your full potential.
