Getting started from the USA

HOW IT WORKS

  1. STEP1

    Sign up or talk first

    Choose your package and sign up straight away, or book a free consultation with Matteo first: the booking page shows the times in your own time zone.

  2. STEP2

    Testing & zones

    Every package starts with tests (CP, FTP, FTHR or INSCYD) to set your zones. On Advanced and Premium, Matteo also does a deep assessment of your last 6–12 months of training data.

  3. STEP3

    Your plan

    Sessions land in Vekta or TrainingPeaks and sync to your Garmin, Wahoo, Hammerhead or Zwift.

  4. STEP4

    Feedback

    On Advanced and Premium, Matteo reviews your sessions (weekly or daily). Feedback and changes are written in Vekta or TrainingPeaks, so you read them when it suits you.

  5. STEP5

    Nutrition, if you want it

    Add 1-to-1 nutrition coaching with our sports nutritionist, Marine Lenehan (WorldTour Pro): daily macros, carbs per hour and notes in the same calendar as your training.

FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS

Which package is right for me?

It depends on your budget and how much direct feedback you want. Basic ($264, one payment for 12 weeks) is a plan you follow on your own. Advanced ($179/month) adds Matteo: weekly feedback and schedule changes, a monthly call and Vekta membership, and it is our most popular package. Premium is the closest coaching, with daily contact; it is full at the moment, but you can join the waiting list.

I'm based in the USA — does coaching work remotely?

Yes. All coaching is delivered online — plans, feedback, testing and communication happen asynchronously via Vekta or Training Peaks, so you never need to be online at the same time. Calls are booked at a time that suits you, whether you’re on the East Coast, West Coast, or anywhere between.

Do you only coach people who race?

No. We coach riders working toward weight loss, their first century ride, RAGBRAI, general fitness, Gran Fondos, gravel, triathlon or racing — the only requirement is that you ride a bike and want to improve.

Do I need to switch from Training Peaks to Vekta?

No — we can continue working in Training Peaks if you’re already comfortable there, or explore Vekta’s Critical Power and W′ analytics alongside it.

What age do I need to be?

We coach all ages, 14 and up — no upper limit. Riders under U23 receive a 20% student discount, which also covers juniors racing USA Cycling events.

My event is months away — can I still sign up now?

Yes, and earlier is better: a summer target like RAGBRAI in late July or a fall century gives you time for a base block, a build block and a taper. Pick the package that fits the time you have.

Can you coach me for a long gravel race?

Yes. Gravel and MTB are part of what we coach. Long gravel races are won or lost on pacing and fuelling as much as fitness: Advanced and Premium include daily carb recommendations for each session and a race and pacing strategy for the event.

Can I work 1-to-1 with a nutritionist?

Yes. You can add nutrition coaching with our sports nutritionist, Marine Lenehan: a WorldTour Pro with LIDL-TREK, 2× Irish National Champion and Amateur Gravel World Champion 2024. She writes your daily macros, carbs per hour and nutrition notes into the same calendar as your training, reviews your food diary and plans your race fuelling and gut training. The price depends on what you need, and is agreed with Marine.
