USING VEKTA - NEXT GENERATION TRAINING & COACHING PLATFORM

With my coaching service, powered by Vekta, you get more than a plan — you get a system that helps you understand your body, optimize your training, and achieve results faster.

Vekta goes far beyond traditional metrics like FTP, using Critical Power (CP) and W′ modelling to measure your true performance capacity, track endurance under fatigue, and guide every effort.

Your training will be precise and purposeful thanks to:

  • Adaptive training zones: Every workout is calibrated to your current fitness, so you’re always training at the right intensity.
  • AI-powered modelling: Advanced algorithms analyze your sessions to identify trends, strengths, and areas to improve.
  • Beyond FTP metrics: Understand your true power, endurance, and recovery capacity with insights used by professional athletes.
  • Personalized guidance: Plans are tailored to your goals, lifestyle, and schedule, helping you stay consistent and motivated.
  • Elite-level tools trusted by pros: Vekta is used by WorldTour teams, giving you access to the same science that powers elite performance.

With this approach, every ride, interval, and effort has purpose. You’ll see clear progress, stay motivated, and know exactly how to push yourself safely toward your personal best.

Good to know

FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS

What would the most suitable package for me?

It depends on your needs: budget, tools available and how much feedback you want to get. The main difference between packages is the level of feedback and communication with the coach. If you are a person that is able to follow the training without the human aspect of a coach, then the Basic will be enough. However, the Advanced Package is the most popular because not only you have access to Matteo as you coach with feedback, phone calls, training analysis, but because you get Vekta membership included worth €19.99/month.

Do you only coach people who race?

No. We coach all sorts of people. You may be interested in losing weight, trying to complete a 100 mile ride, being fitter, completing a sportive, taking part in a triathlon, or bike-racing. All that we ask is you ride a bike, and want to improve in some way.

What is Vekta?

Vektais one of the leading online training platforms, giving you the same tools professional riders use. Follow your plan interactively, track every session, and communicate directly with your coach. Access advanced metrics like Critical Power, W′, and beyond-FTP insights, adaptive training zones, and detailed performance analytics — all from your computer or mobile app. Sync your rides from Garmin, Zwift, Wahoo, or Android, and make every ride count toward your goals.

I am used to Training Peaks, do I need to make the switch to Vekta or can we work also on Training Peaks?

You don’t need to switch if you’re already comfortable with TrainingPeaks — we can absolutely continue working there. That said, Vekta offers some additional features that can give you deeper insights, like Critical Power and W′ metrics, adaptive training zones, and advanced analytics, all presented in a clear, interactive way. So if you’re curious, we can explore Vekta together alongside your current setup — but the most important thing is that your training stays consistent and effective, no matter the platform.

Where the 1to1 sessions take place?

The 1to1 sssions take place in Wicklow Town, Co.Wicklow.

How many hours do I need to follow the plan?

It depends by your training availability.
We hand-build your plan only for you and to suits your needs. Your life commitments are important and we design the plan around them. If you have 5 hours a week available to train, we will schedule the training for 5 hours. If you have 10 hours, we will schedule 10 hours and so on.

What age do need to be to be coached?

We coach all ages. From 14 years old to….wait there is no limit. One of our past coached riders was 75 years old.
We have a 20% student discount for all people below U23.

My event is not until 16 weeks from now, can I still sign up now to the Basic Package?

Yes, of course. You will receive everything that you need when you sign up. The plan should be followed for the 12 weeks before the event. Therefore, even if you sign up a bit earlier, you can still take advantage of the nutritional guide, core and stretching exercises, training and lifestyle analysis, nutritional and time management assessment.
