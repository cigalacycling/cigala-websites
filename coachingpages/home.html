Why Cigala Cycling

WHAT WE OFFER

Cigala Cycling provides premium cycling and triathlon coaching for all levels of cyclist, from leisure to professional.
No matter where you are in the world, we coach remotely in different countries, developing customised coaching plans to suit your needs.

Cigala Cycling was founded by Italian Head Coach Matteo Cigala, that with over 20 years experience in cycling, hundreds of victories in his name and a Diploma in Science and Sport, had a vision of connecting Ireland to the Italian cycling scene, bringing the most advanced and up-to date coaching through his continuous collaboration with Italian professional cyclists and coaches.

With Cigala Cycling Tours we also hold Bike Tours all over Europe such as Lake Garda in Italy, the Algarve in Portugal and in the Italian Alps, French Alps, Pyrenees, Croatia and much more
