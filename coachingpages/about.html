Matteo Cigala is a cycling coach based in Wicklow, Ireland. With 28 years of experience in cycling, over a decade of coaching, hundreds of race victories, and formal qualifications including a Business Management Degree (BA) and a Diploma in Science and Sports, Matteo has built a reputation as one of the leading cycling coaches in Ireland.

In 2015, he brought his vision to life: connecting Ireland to the Italian cycling scene and delivering some of the most advanced, up-to-date coaching methods.

Through continuous collaboration with professional cyclists, WorldTour teams, top-level sport sciences and coaches, he ensures that his athletes benefit from the same performance standards used at the highest level of the sport.

Today, Matteo is widely regarded as the most successful cycling coach in Ireland of the last decade, coaching athletes of all levels — from beginners to competitive club riders to WorldTour professionals.

Matteo’s story

MATTEO'S STORY

  1. FROM ITALIAN TALENT TO INTERNATIONAL COMPETITOR

    He began racing at the age of five, quickly rising through the Italian cycling system and establishing himself as one of the most talented young riders of his generation.

    As a junior, Matteo represented Italy on the world stage, participating in some of the most prestigious races in global cycling:

    • World Championship Juniors 2011 — Copenhagen
    • Paris–Roubaix Juniors 2011 — France
    • Trofeo Karlsberg 2011 — Germany
    • Course de la Paix 2011 (Czech Republic) — podium in Stage 5

    His early career highlights also include:

    • Winner — Giro della Lunigiana Stage 4 (2010)
    • Winner — GP Eco di Bergamo
    • Multiple-time Lombardia Regional Champion

    These achievements positioned him among Italy’s strongest junior talents.

  2. THE IRISH CHAPTER — DOMINANCE, CONSISTENCY & LEADERSHIP

    After moving to Ireland, Matteo became one of the nation’s most successful cyclists.

    • 2016–2024

      During the Rás Tailteann (UCI 2.2), Matteo finished as Top County Rider 20 times across five editions (2016–2024), including six podiums — a record unmatched by any other rider.

    • 2017

      In 2017, he finished the season as Ireland’s highest-ranking Elite domestic rider, winning the Cycling Ireland A1 Classification.

    • 2018

      In 2018, Matteo raced professionally with the Holdsworth Pro Racing Team, competing in major international events including the Tour of Qinghai Lake (UCI 2.HC) — China’s biggest stage race

    • 2018

      That same period brought a historic achievement: alongside his athlete Greg Swinand, Matteo broke the Irish Hour Record twice — first in Colorado, then in Aguascalientes, Mexico with a distance of 48.469 km.

    • 2019–2020

      ​From 2019 to 2020, Matteo served as Sports Director and Head of Athlete Performance at Ireland’s only UCI professional team, EvoPro Racing, further expanding his expertise in elite performance management.

  3. A NEW ERA — EUROPEAN CHAMPION, WORLD CLASS RESULTS

    The years that followed cemented Matteo as one of Europe’s most successful amateur riders:

    2021

    Season Highlights

    • Ranked Top 5–10 in the Zwift E-Sports World Ranking
    • Won 11 road races in 4 months, including major European events:
      • Gran Fondo Il Lombardia
      • Gran Fondo Tre Valli Varesine
      • Medio Fondo Gran Fondo Strade Bianche
      • Medio Fondo Gran Fondo Colnago
    2022

    Season Highlights

    • Represented Italy at the UCI eSports World Championship, finishing 25th
    • Won the Medio Fondo at Gran Fondo Strade Bianche for the second consecutive year
    • Achieved 8 consecutive road wins
    • At the Rás Tailteann, finished on the podium 3 times and 2nd in the points classification
    • Achieved the most prestigious result of his amateur career: European Road Masters Champion (19–34) — Reggio Emilia, Italy
    • Finished 6th at the first-ever UCI Gravel World Championship
    2023

    Season Highlights

    • Silver Medal — UCI Gran Fondo World Championship
    • Silver Medal — UCI Gravel World Championship
    • Winner — Italian National Gravel Championship
    2024, 2025

    Season Highlights

    • Won the County Rider Classification in Ras Tailteann
    • 4th at the Gravel & 5th in the Gran Fondo World Championship
    • Winner of Volta Almodovar
    • Winner of Stage 1 of Ras Mumhan, 8 years after his first win in the same race
