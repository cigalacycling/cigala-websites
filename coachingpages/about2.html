WHY CHOOSE OUR TRIATHLON COACHING?

Whether you’re training for your first sprint triathlon or aiming for a personal best in Ironman, Cigala Cycling Coaching provides the structured, personalised support you need to reach your full potential.

See our triathlon coaching packages

Led by former professional cyclist and elite-level coach Matteo Cigala, our triathlon coaching program blends world-class cycling expertise with proven endurance training principles to help athletes get fitter, faster, and more confident across all three disciplines.

✔ Personalised Training Plans
Your plan is tailored to your schedule, fitness level, goals, and race calendar — no generic templates, ever.

✔ Expert Cycling Foundation
Improve your strongest discipline or fix your weakest. With Matteo’s pro cycling background, you get unmatched guidance on power, pacing, and race strategy.

✔ Full Triathlon Approach
We support all three disciplines — swim, bike, run — with balanced weekly programming and progression.

✔ Data-Driven Coaching

Training is structured around: Power and heart-rate metric; Periodisation, Fueling strategy, Recovery optimisation.

✔ Dedicated Support

Regular check-ins, feedback on workouts, open communication, and guidance to keep you accountable and progressing.

✔ For All Levels

From beginners to advanced age-group or pro athletes — everyone is welcome and supported.
