Privacy Policy

  1. Who we are

    This website, coaching.cigalacycling.com, is run by Cigala Cycling Limited, a company based in Co. Wicklow, Ireland ("we", "us"). We are the controller of the personal data described in this policy.

    For any privacy question or request, contact us at info@cigalacycling.com or +353 404 78107.

    Our other websites (cigalacycling.com, tours.cigalacycling.com and retail.cigalacycling.com) each have their own privacy policy covering the services and tools they use. By using this website you confirm that you have read this policy.

  2. What we collect and why

    Contact form: your name, email address and message. We use them only to answer your enquiry. Legal basis: your request and our legitimate interest in replying to it.

    Free consultation: when you book a call through Calendly, your name, email address and any notes you add. We use them to hold the call. Legal basis: your request.

    Coaching: if you sign up, your name, contact details, goals, training availability, race calendar and the training data from your devices and apps (for example power, heart rate, speed and GPS rides). We use them to build, adjust and analyse your training plan. Legal basis: our contract with you.

    Health information: some coaching services use information about your health, such as injuries, medical conditions or the blood panel you share for review by our sports nutritionist. We only use it to coach you safely, only with your explicit consent, and you can withdraw that consent at any time.

    Payments: packages are paid through Shopify (one-off payments) or GoCardless (monthly Direct Debit). They process your payment details; we receive only the confirmation and your contact details.

    Live chat: the messages and any details you choose to share in the chat. We use them to answer you. Legal basis: your request and our legitimate interest in replying to it.

    Website analytics and advertising: information about how you use the site, collected only if you accept cookies (see "Cookies" below). Legal basis: your consent.

    We do not store or maintain credit card details, nor do we share customer financial details with any third parties.

  3. Cookies

    When you first visit, a banner asks whether you accept cookies. Until you choose, and if you reject, we do not set analytics or advertising cookies.

    If you accept, we use:

    • Google Analytics 4, provided by Google Ireland Limited, to understand how visitors use the site. Google Consent Mode keeps it in a cookie-free state until you accept.
    • Meta Pixel, provided by Meta Platforms Ireland Limited, to measure and improve our Facebook and Instagram advertising.
    • The OpenWidget live chat, provided by Text S.A. (Poland), which loads automatically after you accept. If you have not accepted, the chat only loads when you click "Chat with us".

    Some pages show reviews from our athletes through a Shapo widget, which loads from Shapo's servers.

    We also store your cookie choice in your browser (local storage), so we don't ask you again.

    You can change your choice at any time with the "Cookie settings" link at the bottom of every page.

  4. Who receives your data

    We only share personal data with the service providers that help us run the site and our coaching:

    • Hostinger, our website host (EU).
    • Google Ireland Limited (Google Analytics, and Google Fonts, which delivers the website's fonts and receives your IP address when a page loads).
    • Meta Platforms Ireland Limited (Meta Pixel, only if you accept cookies).
    • Text S.A. (OpenWidget live chat).
    • Calendly (free consultation bookings).
    • Shopify and GoCardless (payments).
    • Vekta and TrainingPeaks, the training platforms where your plan and training data are kept, if you are coached.
    • Skool, if you join our online community.
    • Shapo (athlete reviews shown on the site).
    • Our sports nutritionist and mental coach, when their service is part of your package.

    Some of these providers may transfer data outside the European Economic Area, for example to the United States. Where they do, they use legal safeguards approved by the European Commission, such as the EU-US Data Privacy Framework or Standard Contractual Clauses.

    We never sell your personal data.

  5. How long we keep it

    Contact form and chat enquiries: for as long as needed to deal with your enquiry, and no longer than two years afterwards.

    Coaching records: for as long as we coach you, and no longer than two years after your coaching ends. Invoices and payment records are kept for as long as Irish tax law requires.

    Health information: deleted when your coaching ends, or earlier if you withdraw your consent.

    Analytics data: kept in Google Analytics in line with our retention settings, after which it is deleted automatically.

  6. How we protect your data

    The website uses an encrypted connection (HTTPS). Access to our website, email, payment and training platform accounts is restricted and password-protected, and we only work with reputable providers that apply their own security measures.

    No method of sending or storing data over the internet is completely secure. We take reasonable steps to protect your data, but we cannot guarantee absolute security, and you share information with us at your own risk. If a data breach is likely to affect your rights, we will inform you and the Data Protection Commission as the law requires.

  7. Children

    This website is not intended for children under 16. We do not knowingly collect personal data from children under 16 through the website. We coach younger riders (for example in our talent programme) only through a parent or guardian, who agrees to how we use the rider's data. If you believe a child has sent us personal data, contact us and we will delete it.

  8. Other websites and social media

    This website links to other websites and platforms, including our other Cigala Cycling sites, Calendly, Vekta, TrainingPeaks, Skool, WhatsApp, Instagram, Facebook, YouTube, TikTok, X and Threads. When you follow those links or contact us on those platforms, their own privacy policies apply. We are not responsible for the content or privacy practices of websites and platforms we do not control.

  9. Your rights

    Under the GDPR you have the right to:

    • access the personal data we hold about you;
    • have it corrected or deleted;
    • object to or restrict how we use it;
    • receive it in a portable format;
    • withdraw your consent at any time (for example through "Cookie settings", or by telling us you no longer want us to use your health information).

    To use any of these rights, email info@cigalacycling.com. We will reply within one month.

    If you are not happy with how we handle your data, you can complain to the Data Protection Commission in Ireland (www.dataprotection.ie).

  10. Governing law

    This policy is governed by the laws of Ireland. Nothing in it limits any rights you have under data protection law.

  11. Changes to this policy

    We may update this policy when our website or services change. The latest version is always on this page.

    Last updated: November 2026.
