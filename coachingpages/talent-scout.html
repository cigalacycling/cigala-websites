Our aim

We are a Talent Scout

Our aim is to identify the next professional cyclists and help them step up to the next level, thanks to our collaboration with many professional teams as well as A&J AllSports.

It is a coaching relationship with Head Coach Matteo Cigala with the aim of helping young riders (from the age of 15 to 22 years old) achieve their dream of becoming a professional cyclist.

Matteo Cigala is a talent scout for A&J AllSports, the agency of some of the best riders in the WorldTour, such as Tadej Pogačar, Isaac Del Toro, Biniam Girmay and Jasper Philipsen. On the programme you get world-class coaching from him, and when you are ready, the agency and the teams hear about you.

How we find talent

THE DATA BEHIND THE TALENT

Turning pro is not just about numbers, but the numbers open the door. Matteo analyses every ride and race file to see where a rider is today, how fast they are improving, and how far they are from the level the teams look for.

  • Power profile

    Best power from a 5-second sprint to an hour, compared with what riders at the next level produce.

  • Critical Power & W′

    How much you can sustain, and how many hard efforts you have in the tank.

  • Durability

    The power you still have after hours of racing: where races are won.

  • Race files

    Every race analysed: where you lost time, where you can win.

  • Recovery & load

    Training load, sleep and recovery, so you improve without burning out.

  • Year-on-year progression

    The numbers that show a team you are on the way up.

  • Pacing & race tactics

    How you spend your energy in a race: when to sit in, when to go, and how to finish the job.

  • Physiology testing

    INSCYD metabolic testing: VO2max, VLamax, fat and carb use, so training targets what holds you back.

A&J AllSports

TALENT SCOUT

A&J AllSports is a Sport Management Agency, founded by Alex and Johnny Carera, that works with hundreds of top athletes from cycling, tennis, triathlon, swimming etc.

A&J's focus and goals are to take care of the athlete's career path, negotiate and manage their contracts with the top teams in each disciplines or sport.

Cigala Cycling is proud to work with A&J as talent scout, ensuring that talented riders can move to the best possible set up for their career, reaching the World Tour level.

Our goal is to move talented riders from local level to Conti teams in Europe to then reach the maximum level of sport, the World Tour level.

Good to know

FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS

What is the Cigala Cycling Talent Programme?

It is a coaching relationship with Head Coach Matteo Cigala with the aim of helping young riders (from the age of 15 to 22 years old) achieve their dream of becoming a professional cyclist.

How can the programme help me?

We have limited availability and for young athletes to be followed by Head Coach Matteo Cigala is a privileged position.
Matteo works closely with some of the biggest elite and pro teams in the world as well as A&J AllSports.
Matteo will help you to achieve the numbers required to get to the pro level, not only from the training point of view but with all aspect of cycling. Turning pro is not just about numbers, it is also about who you know and who you work with, being in the right place at the right time.

What do I need to do to get started?

Please contact us at info@cigalacycling.com to state your interest in the programme, and, if we have availability we will send you the link to sign up to get started.
Please note that a video call or a face to face interview might be requested before getting started.

What's the cost?

We offer 20% discount from our individual packages to underage, juniors and U23.
For the Cigala Cycling talent programme we only offer the Advanced Package at €119.20 a month (20% off €149). See the Advanced Package

Does the programme include nutrition?

Yes, if you want it. You can add 1-to-1 nutrition coaching with our sports nutritionist, Marine Lenehan: a WorldTour Pro with LIDL-TREK, 2× Irish National Champion and Amateur Gravel World Champion 2024. She writes your daily macros, carbs per hour and nutrition notes into the same calendar as your training, reviews your food diary and builds your race fuelling and gut training. Read more about nutrition coaching

What does my training calendar look like?

Every session is planned in your calendar on Vekta, synced to your devices and apps, with the targets for the day. If you work with Marine, her nutrition notes and carbs per hour sit next to each session (see the example above). After each ride you add your RPE and a comment, so Matteo can adapt the plan.

Who are A&J AllSports?

A&J AllSports is the sports management agency of Alex and Johnny Carera, founded in 1997. It represents some of the best riders in the WorldTour, such as Tadej Pogačar, Isaac Del Toro, Biniam Girmay and Jasper Philipsen, and in the past Chris Froome and Vincenzo Nibali.

Can I stay with my club or team?

Yes. The programme is your coaching: you keep racing with your club or team, and Matteo plans your training around your race calendar.

Does joining guarantee a professional contract?

No. Nobody can promise that. What we promise is world-class coaching, an honest analysis of your numbers, and that when a rider is ready, the right people hear about it.
