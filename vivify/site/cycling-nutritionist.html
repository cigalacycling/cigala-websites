Fuel like a pro

A cycling nutritionist who races at the top

Most nutritionists learn cycling from the outside. Marine fuels her own WorldTour racing with LIDL-TREK using the same evidence-based strategies she builds for her clients. She knows what a five-hour training day, a crit or a gravel race asks of the body. She also knows what you can actually eat from a jersey pocket.

Whether you are training for the Wicklow 200, your first gravel race or a season of racing, your plan follows your training load week by week. Marine works with TrainingPeaks, Vekta and MyFitnessPal, so you know what to eat on and off the bike.

Elite & pro riders

Elite and professional riders

Marine races in the WorldTour with LIDL-TREK, so she knows the demands of elite cycling from the inside: a full race calendar, stage races, travel and the fine margins that decide results. Elite and development riders get the same evidence-based approach she uses for her own racing.

Cycling nutrition

Common questions

How many carbs per hour should I eat on the bike?

It depends on how long and how hard you ride. For long rides and endurance events, 60–90 g of carbohydrate per hour is a common target, and some athletes reach 100–120 g/hour after training their gut to tolerate it. Marine explains the details in How Many Carbs Per Hour Do You Need?

Can you help me lose weight without losing power?

Yes. Marine’s approach is “fuel for performance, not restriction”: enough energy and carbohydrate to support training, adequate protein, hydration and recovery, without extreme diets. One client lost over 13 kg while getting stronger for the Traka gravel race.

Do you work with gravel and ultra-distance riders?

Yes. Marine is the Amateur Gravel World Champion (2024) and Irish National Gravel Champion (2026), and has coached riders for ultra-endurance gravel races.

Can you help me prepare for a Gran Fondo or sportive?

Yes. Marine is the European Gran Fondo Champion (2023). She builds your daily nutrition around your training and a race-day plan for your event, whether that is the Wicklow 200, L’Étape du Tour, the Marmotte or a UCI Gran Fondo World Series race.

Do you work with elite and professional riders?

Yes. Marine races in the WorldTour with LIDL-TREK and works with riders up to elite level: race calendars and stage races, high-carbohydrate race fuelling, safe body composition, training camps and batch-tested supplements.

Do I need to be in Ireland?

No. Coaching is online. Marine currently coaches athletes in the USA, Canada, Ireland and Finland, and has coached athletes in France, Italy, the Netherlands and Austria. Plans are priced in euro and US dollars.
