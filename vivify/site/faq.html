Your questions

What does a sports nutritionist do?

A sports nutritionist helps you eat for your training and your goals: enough energy and carbohydrate for the work you do, protein for recovery, hydration, and fuelling during sessions and races. Marine also looks at vitamins, minerals and supplements, gut health and your relationship with food.

Who is Marine Lenehan?

Marine is the founder of Vivify Sports. She holds an MSc in Sports Nutrition from Universidad Europea de Madrid and has raced as a WorldTour professional with LIDL-TREK since 2025. Read more about Marine.

Do I need to live in Ireland?

No. Coaching is online and Marine works with athletes worldwide. Prices are shown in euro and US dollars. See online coaching.

Which plan should I choose?

Start with the Nutritional Assessment for a one-off review and report. For ongoing support, The Healthy Body Plan builds habits with a weekly macro plan, The Happy Body Plan adds a weekly meal plan and daily food diary analysis, and The Performing Body Plan adds unlimited calls, daily WhatsApp support and sport-specific work such as a competition plan and sweat rate test. Compare the plans.

How much do the plans cost?

The Nutritional Assessment is 100€ (120 US$) and a Follow-up Consultation is 50€ (60 US$). The 3-month plans are 99€, 149€ or 199€ a month (119, 180 or 240 US$).

Is the discovery call free?

Yes. It is a free 30-minute call to talk about your goals and see whether coaching is right for you. Book it here.

How many carbs per hour should I eat during endurance exercise?

For long endurance sessions and events, 60–90 g per hour is a common target, and trained athletes can reach 100–120 g/hour after gut training. Read the full article.

Do you work with clubs and teams?

Yes. Marine gives workshops and talks for clubs, gyms and teams. See Workshops & Talks.

Can you help with weight loss?

Yes, without crash diets. Clients have lost weight while gaining energy and power. The focus is on sustainable habits and fuelling your training properly.
