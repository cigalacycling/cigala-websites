DEEP TISSUE MASSAGE
SPORTS MASSAGE

A MASSAGE FOR WHO?

For tense women and men, who suffer from muscle pain, cramps, tension, tightness or who seek comfort in their joints. Ideal after sports training. Using a sports massage regularly to relieve tight muscles can help reduce the chance of injury and maximise performance.

WITH WHAT RESULTS?

This massage, which combines firmness and softness, focuses on your deep muscles and contracted tissues by enhancing blood circulation through tight muscles, breaking down inflexible tissues and stretching out muscle fibers. The purpose of Deep Tissue Massage is to “unstick” the glued fibres of a muscle while releasing the build-up of tension, and eliminate toxins while relaxing and soothing the muscle itself

"Restorative method, optimizing your recovery"

CUPPING THERAPY

A MASSAGE FOR WHO?

For women and men, looking to detoxify their body by releasing the toxins present in bodily tissues and organs. Ideal to reduce muscle recovery time.

WITH WHAT RESULTS?

This Dry Cupping technique alleviates muscle pain, tension and tightness. Especially recommended for :

  • Arthritis
  • Back pain, neck pain, knee pain and shoulder pain.
  • Optimizing muscle recovery.
  • Breathing problems, such as asthma.
  • Gastrointestinal disorders, such as irritable bowel disease (IBD).
  • Headaches and migraines.
  • High blood pressure (hypertension)

"A healthy outside, starts from the inside"
