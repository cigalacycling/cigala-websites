From Wicklow to the world

Coaching that travels with you

Vivify Sports is based in Wicklow, Ireland, and works with athletes worldwide. Everything happens online: consultations by video call, your plan updated weekly from your training load, and support between calls.

Marine races the WorldTour herself, so she understands travel and racing abroad, and fits your nutrition around them.

How it works
  • Free 30-minute discovery call
  • Nutritional assessment by video call
  • Weekly plan from your TrainingPeaks or Vekta training load
  • Food diary review in MyFitnessPal

Working online

Common questions

How do online consultations work?

Consultations are private online video calls. Between calls, Marine follows your training on TrainingPeaks or Vekta and your food diary on MyFitnessPal, and updates your plan as your training changes.

Can I pay in US dollars?

Yes. Every option is priced in euro and US dollars. For example, the Nutritional Assessment is 100€ / 120 US$.

Which athletes do you work with?

Endurance athletes, mainly cyclists, triathletes and runners, from first events to elite level. Marine currently coaches athletes in the USA, Canada, Ireland and Finland, and has coached athletes in France, Italy and the Netherlands.

Can I try it before committing?

Yes. The first step is a free 30-minute discovery call.
