Privacy Policy

VIVIFY SPORTS PRIVACY POLICY

Last updated: 19 November 2026

1. Who we are

Vivify Sports is the sports nutrition business of Marine Lenehan, based in Wicklow, Ireland (VAT IE8879803O). Marine is responsible for the personal data described in this policy (the "data controller"). You can contact her at marine@vivifysports.com or +353 87 224 7402.

2. What information we collect

  • Contact form: your name, email address, the service you are interested in and your message.
  • Bookings and payments: when you book a call or buy a plan or the e-book, you enter your details with our booking and payment providers (see section 4). We receive your name, email address, what you booked or bought and the payment confirmation. We never see your full card or bank details.
  • Coaching clients: the information you share with Marine for your coaching, such as your goals, training, food diary, measurements and health information. Health information is sensitive data: we only use it to provide your coaching, with your explicit consent.
  • Technical data: our website host keeps server logs (IP address, browser and pages requested) to keep the website secure.

3. Why we use it

  • To reply to your messages and provide the services you ask for (to perform a contract, or to take steps at your request before one).
  • To deliver your coaching, including health information, with your explicit consent, which you can withdraw at any time.
  • To keep accounting and tax records (a legal obligation).
  • To keep the website secure and working (our legitimate interest).

We do not sell your data, and we do not send you marketing emails without your consent.

4. Who we share it with

We use trusted providers who process data on our behalf, only for the services below:

  • Hostinger: website hosting. The website runs on WordPress, installed on our hosting.
  • Cal.com: booking calls and consultations.
  • Stripe and GoCardless: online payments (e-book, consultations and monthly plans).
  • Google: email, the Google reviews shown on our pages and the fonts used on the website.
  • Trustindex: the tool that displays our Google reviews.
  • The coaching tools you choose to use with Marine, such as TrainingPeaks, Vekta, MyFitnessPal and WhatsApp.

Some of these providers may process data outside the European Economic Area. When they do, they use recognised safeguards such as the European Commission’s standard contractual clauses.

5. Cookies

Our website does not use advertising or analytics cookies. Only cookies needed for the website to work may be set. When you book or pay, Cal.com, Stripe and GoCardless use their own cookies on their pages. If we add website statistics in the future, we will ask for your consent first.

6. How long we keep it

  • Messages from the contact form: up to 2 years.
  • Coaching records: for the length of your coaching and up to 2 years after, unless you ask us to delete them sooner.
  • Accounting records: 6 years, as required by Irish law.

7. Your rights

Under the GDPR you can ask to access, correct or delete your data, to receive a copy of it, to restrict or object to its use, and to withdraw your consent at any time. Just email marine@vivifysports.com. You can also complain to the Data Protection Commission in Ireland (www.dataprotection.ie).

8. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. The date at the top shows the latest version.
