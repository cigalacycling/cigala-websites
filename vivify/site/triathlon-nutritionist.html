Three sports, one plan

Fuel your training and your race

Triathlon training is long and varied, often with two sessions in a day. Fuelling it well makes the difference between adapting to training and just getting tired. Marine builds your nutrition week by week from your training load, so hard days are fuelled and recovery is supported.

On race day the bike leg sets up your run. Your plan covers how much carbohydrate and fluid you take on the bike, what you carry on the run and how to train your gut to tolerate it.

What we work on
  • Daily fuelling for double days
  • Bike-to-run fuelling
  • Carbs per hour
  • Hydration & sweat rate
  • Gut training
  • Recovery between sessions
  • Body composition
  • Supplements

Common questions

Can you help me plan nutrition for an Ironman 70.3?

Yes. Marine builds the plan around your training and your race: daily fuelling for three sports, carbohydrate per hour on the bike and run, hydration, and gut training before race day. See her Ironman 70.3 nutrition plan article.

I get stomach problems on the run. Can nutrition help?

Often, yes. Gut health and gut training are part of the coaching: the aim is fewer gastrointestinal problems such as cramping, nausea and bloating, and better carbohydrate absorption.

Do you coach the training too?

Marine coaches nutrition. She works alongside your coach and your training plan, using TrainingPeaks so your fuelling follows your training load.
