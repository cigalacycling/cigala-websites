Sports Nutritionist | Helping Endurance Athletes Fuel for Performance

Hi, I'm Marine Lenehan, founder of Vivify Sports, a sports nutritionist based in Wicklow, Ireland, working with athletes worldwide.

My passion for sports nutrition comes from two worlds that have shaped my life: competitive sport and nutrition science.

As a professional WorldTour cyclist and a qualified sports nutritionist, I understand the demands of endurance training from both sides. I know what it takes to prepare for competition, manage demanding training schedules, fuel long sessions and prioritize recovery.

My goal is to turn sports nutrition science into practical strategies that help athletes feel stronger, perform better and get more out of their training

From the Athletics Track to the WorldTour

As a longtime athlete, formerly a hurdler and now a professional cyclist, I understand firsthand what it takes to support both the body and the mind through training, racing, and recovery.

In 2025, I signed with LIDL-TREK, marking the moment I officially stepped into the WorldTour as a professional rider. Competing at the highest level has strengthened both my experience and my philosophy as a nutritionist because the strategies I use to fuel myself for the WorldTour racing are the same evidence-based tools I bring to the athletes I support. I’m still proud to hold titles as:

  • Irish National Gravel Champion (2026)
  • Amateur Gravel World Champion (2024)
  • Amateur Gravel European Champion (2023,2024)
  • Irish National E-Sport Champion (2024, 2025)
  • Irish National Criterium Champion (2024)
  • European Gran Fondo Champion (2023)
  • Podium Elite Irish Road Nationals (2025)
  • Podium Elite Irish TT Nationals (2026)

But more than medals, what drives me is helping others discover their own version of success through personalised coaching that balances both science and individuality.

Education & Qualifications

I’ve spent years building a well-rounded foundation to support the athletes I coach. My qualifications include:

  • MSc in Sports Nutrition – Universidad Europea de Madrid (2025)
  • Professional Diploma in Sports Nutrition – Barça Innovation Hub (2023)

These tools enable me to support the entire athlete, not just their diet, but also their movement, mindset, and recovery.

My coaching philosophy

Elite-Level Experience, Practical Nutrition Coaching

As a professional WorldTour cyclist, I understand the physical demands of endurance sport first-hand. I know how important nutrition is in supporting demanding training schedules, improving recovery and preparing for competition. But good sports nutrition isn't just about what works in theory. It needs to work in real life.

At Vivify Sports, I combine evidence-based nutrition with practical experience to create personalized strategies that fit your training, goals, lifestyle and individual needs.

My coaching philosophy is built around three principles:

1.Evidence-based, individualised nutrition

Every athlete is different. Your nutrition plan should reflect your training load, performance goals, nutritional requirements and personal preferences.

2.Fuel for performance, not restriction

Eating enough to support training is essential. I focus on appropriate energy and carbohydrate intake, adequate protein, hydration and recovery, without unnecessary food restrictions or extreme approaches.

3.Sustainable habits and practical strategies

Nutrition should complement your life, not complicate it. I help athletes develop realistic habits and practical fueling strategies they can maintain throughout training, competition and everyday life.

Whether you're preparing for your first endurance event or competing at an advanced level, my goal is to give you the knowledge, confidence and nutritional support to perform at your best.

My aim is to make nutrition easier to understand, easier to implement and more effective in everyday life.

“You are unique, simply and wonderfully you.”

Your uniqueness is your power. I believe in working with, not against, your body. Together, we’ll uncover what energizes you, makes you feel strong, and keeps you in sync with your goals.
