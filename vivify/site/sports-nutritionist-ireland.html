PERSONALIZED NUTRITION PLANS FOR YOU

As an athlete, you want to train hard, compete and be successful. Nutrition will help you do that. Your nutrition requirements are determined by your sport, your training & your genetic make-up.These coaching plans are designed to give you personalized support to reach your goals. Partnered with Training Peaks, Vekta & MyFitnessPal, Marine will build your nutrition plan weekly according to your training load. You will know exactly what to eat on & off training. Whether you are looking to clean up your diet a bit, increase performance, support training adaptations, support your gut health, boost your energy levels or simply have a healthy relationship to food, VIVIFY SPORTS is here to help.

By assessing your dietary patterns, relationship to food, macronutrient & micronutrient balance, VIVIFY SPORTS will give you tailored solutions to reach your goals.

What we look at
  • Macronutrients requirements
  • Vitamins & Minerals requirements
  • Supplements requirements
  • Sports specific requirements
  • Lifestyle advice
  • Healthy recipes
  • Craving control guidelines
  • IBS or gastro-intestinal issues
  • Immune health
An example week

What your coaching looks like

Train like a pro, with everything in one place: Marine works from your training calendar, so your training and your nutrition are planned together.

  • One calendar

    Your nutrition sits next to your training in TrainingPeaks or Vekta: daily macros and carbs, written for the sessions you are doing that day.

  • Planned from your training load

    Marine sees your sessions and adjusts your plan as your training changes: more carbs on the big days, less on the easy ones.

  • Food diary review

    Log what you eat in MyFitnessPal. Marine reviews your diary and sends suggestions.

  • Race & event fuelling

    A fuelling strategy for each race, with gut training so your stomach can take it on the day.
