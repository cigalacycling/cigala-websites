Common questions

When should I get my fuelling plan?

Ideally several weeks before your event, so you can practise it in training and train your gut to tolerate the carbohydrate you will need on the day.

Is a fuelling plan included in the coaching plans?

A competition nutrition plan is part of The Performing Body Plan (sold out for now). You can also buy a fuelling plan on its own for a single event, for 70€.

How much does a fuelling plan cost?

70€. It includes a 30-minute call with Marine to understand your event, followed by your personal fuelling plan as a PDF. You pay online when you buy it.

Which events do you plan for?

Endurance events of any length: road races, sportives, gravel and ultra-distance rides, triathlon from sprint to Ironman, and running races.
