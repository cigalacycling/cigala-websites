Pesenti rental sync

Internal files for the scheduled check that keeps the Cicli Pesenti bike-rental page up to date. Keep this artifact private.

  • sync.py: reads bookings from WeTravel, finds bike rentals on tours in Italy, France, Slovenia, Croatia and Switzerland, and writes the page and the Italian email.
  • page_template.txt: the layout of the shared page.
