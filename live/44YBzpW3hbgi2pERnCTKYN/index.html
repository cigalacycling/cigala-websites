Italy's Coast to Coast

Tyrrhenian Marina di Pietrasanta → Pisa → Siena → Assisi → San Benedetto del Tronto Adriatic
25/09 – 02/10

Sea to sea

Ride across Italy from the Tyrrhenian Sea to the Adriatic, starting in Marina di Pietrasanta and finishing in San Benedetto del Tronto.

The route takes you through some of central Italy’s most beautiful landscapes and historic destinations, including Pisa, Siena and Assisi. Ride through the rolling hills of Tuscany, cross the Apennines and discover picturesque villages, quiet country roads and spectacular mountain scenery.

From the Tuscan coast to the heart of Umbria and finally to the Adriatic, this is a true Italian cycling adventure combining challenging riding, culture, history and incredible food.
