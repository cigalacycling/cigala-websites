How to read the numbers
Windows
The 7-day window is the last 7 days including today (the day the page is run). Sessions, nights and plans fall on the athlete's own local date. The previous week is the 7 days before it.
Volume, time, distance
Volume 7d adds up Vekta's own training-load score for every session in the window. Time is moving time without pauses (effective duration). Distance and metres climbed are plain sums. Sessions with no local start date are left out.
Load-8, Load-2, Strain
Load-8 is an 8-week rolling average of daily Vekta volume and Load-2 a 2-week one, both weighted towards recent days (exponential, alpha 2/57 and 2/15). Strain is the 2-week weighted average of daily Vekta intensity, where a day's intensity is the time-weighted mean of its sessions. Rest days count as zero. Each series starts from zero 56 days before the window, the same way the Vekta apps compute them. All three are read at the end of yesterday, so a half-finished today never drags them down. The change shown is over 7 days. The ratio is Load-2 divided by Load-8: above 1x means recent load is running above the long-term level (the overreaching signal), below 1x is normal in a taper or the off season. Ramp is how fast Load-8 has moved per day over the last two days.
Compliance
Planned sessions in the window that Vekta has paired with a completed activity, out of all planned sessions in the window. Riding without a plan does not count against it. A planned session on a past day with no paired activity is marked missed. Hidden plans (the coach's drafts, not on the athlete's devices) are shown as drafts and never counted.
Recovery baselines
The athlete's own 28-day averages of HRV, resting heart rate and sleep duration, leaving out the most recent night. HRV last and Resting HR are that most recent night; when it is older than last night, the tile says which night. Normal is the athlete's own range that the recovery flags use (see below), also drawn on the 30-night charts. Sleep avg is the mean of the nights in the 7-day window (a night belongs to the day the athlete woke up). When two devices recorded the same night, their values are averaged, so the series never jumps between devices that read a few bpm apart; a nap does not count as a night.
Blanks
A blank cell means Vekta has no value, usually because no wearable or power meter was connected. A heart rate of 0 means none was recorded and is never averaged.
Who needs attention
Red triangle: needs action. Amber diamond: worth a look. A plain chip is minor and does not put an athlete on the list. Nothing logged (worth a look): no session in the 7-day window. Everything else comes from the 48-hour checks. Missed and modified look at yesterday's plan (and today's once done, or all of today after 20:00), so each day is judged once. Missed: nothing done that day; a short (45 min or less) or non-endurance session like a stretch routine counts as minor, and mobility or gym in its place counts as done. Modified: a different endurance session done instead, duration outside 70-135% of plan (or work outside 70-130%), or ridden two or more steps away from the planned stimulus. RPE: outside the plan's target ±1.5, or outside the usual range for the planned stimulus (recovery 1-3, aerobic 2-5, tempo 4-7, threshold 6-9, VO2max/anaerobic 7-10); with no plan, Vekta's own classification. Feeling 1-2 of 5 is flagged. Comments: the athlete's latest comment on an activity, written in the window with no reply after it; high priority when it mentions illness, pain, fatigue, a skipped session or asks a question, otherwise minor. Notes: medical, mental, training, nutrition or other notes the athlete wrote for these days; availability and rest-day notes only when a session is still planned that day. Recovery: the last two nights against the athlete's own previous 28 nights; flagged at 1.5 standard deviations the wrong way (low HRV, high resting HR, short sleep). A night recorded by two devices counts once, with their values averaged; a nap is not a night. Ranked by the most serious single item, then by the total: medical/mental notes and worrying comments 5, a missed key session 3, the rest 1-3 by severity. A total of 1 (one minor item) stays on track. A session uploaded twice (same sport, starts within 5 minutes) counts once. An activity not linked to the plan stands in for one planned session that day, same sport first: in the same sport it is judged as that session (modified, RPE), in another sport it is reported as done instead. Plan-template accounts (Gran Fondo 12h, Gran Fondo 16h, Gran Fondo 8h, from planner-config.json skipTemplates) are left out. The Load-2 to Load-8 ratio is shown, never flagged.
