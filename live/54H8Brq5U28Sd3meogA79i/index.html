Your rules · say “change my rules” in the chat to change them.

Look at: Four layers, each against the athlete's own last 28 days.

Physiological: HRV, resting heart rate, skin temperature and sleep from their wearable; Load and Strain; heart rate against power, and whether intervals hit target; CP, W′ and durability after 20 kJ per kilo; weight; left-right balance and cadence on climbs.

Physical: Fatigue and soreness in the morning check-in; RPE and feeling after every session; keywords in the comments they write after sessions: any part of the body they keep mentioning, which side, on or off the bike, after how long, on climbs or the flat, after a change of shoes, cleats, saddle or position, the words getting stronger (niggle, tight, sore, sharp), and anything that sounds like illness, overreaching or under-fuelling.

Emotional: Stress and sleep in the morning check-in; keywords in comments and notes: "want to" turning into "have to", always and never, fixating on the numbers, comparing themselves to others, worrying about letting me down, confidence dropping, exhaustion, pulling away from the group.

Engagement: Missed sessions and late uploads, check-ins stopping, comments getting shorter, RPE left blank, training late at night, doing the bare minimum, nothing planned after a goal event.

One signal on its own goes in their private log.

Two layers, or one signal that keeps getting worse, goes on my watch list.

Three or more layers, or a hard flag, is Act now.

Hard flags: signs of illness before a hard session, and pain described as sharp.

Physiology counts after two nights outside the athlete's normal range.

A check-in counts at 5 or worse on Vekta's 1 to 7 scale (Fatigued, Sore, Bad sleep or Anxious, or worse) three days running, or when it gets worse each day.

Session numbers (Load, Strain, RPE, CP, W′, durability, weight, heart rate against power) count when they are outside the athlete's normal range.

Illness or overreaching before a hard session: an easy session from my library or a rest day, depending on the signal, with a message in my voice.

An injury building: a message asking the athlete for more detail, the next steps for me (a physio where it fits), and a swap for any session that would make it worse.

Fuelling, stress or motivation: no plan change and a message inviting them to talk.

Intervals hitting target: Share of intervals on target below their normal range.

Left-right balance on climbs: Further from 50/50 than their normal range.

Cadence on climbs: Below their normal range.

Feeling after session: Feeling (1 to 5) below their normal range.

Comment keywords: The same body part, or an illness word, in two or more comments within 7 days.

Missed sessions: Planned sessions missed in the last 7 days above their normal range.

Late uploads: Upload delay above their normal range.

Check-ins stopping: No check-in for 3 or more days when they normally check in.

Comments getting shorter: Average comment length in the last 7 days below their normal range.

RPE left blank: Share of sessions with no RPE above their normal range.

Training late at night: Sessions starting at or after 21:00 more often than their normal.

Doing the bare minimum: Session duration as a share of the plan below their normal range.

Nothing planned after a goal event: Goal event within 14 days and nothing planned after it.

CP and W′: CP and W′ count only when outside the normal range and at least 3% below the usual (decided 2026-11-10).

Definitions

Normal range: the athlete's own 28 days (or nights) before the last three, mean ± 1 standard deviation. Week-based signals compare the last 7 days with the 28 days before.

Each signal counts in the direction that signals a problem (HRV, sleep, feeling, CP, W′, durability, cadence lower; resting HR, skin temperature, RPE, Load, Strain higher); weight and power per heartbeat count both ways.

Check-ins: Vekta's 1 (best) to 7 (worst), shown as Vekta's word with the number. Fatigue and Soreness sit in the physical layer, Sleep and Stress in the emotional layer.

Load-8 and Load-2: 8-week and 2-week exponentially weighted averages of daily Vekta volume (alpha 2/57 and 2/15). Strain: the 2-week weighted average of daily Vekta intensity. Each starts from zero 56 days before the window and is read at the end of yesterday. Calculated here, not Vekta's own figures.

Durability: best 20-minute power after 20 kJ/kg as a share of the fresh value, on rides over two hours. Power per heartbeat: average power ÷ average heart rate. Intervals on target: work laps whose average power sits inside the planned range.

Signs of illness: illness words in a comment in the last three days, or skin temperature up together with HRV or resting heart rate outside normal. A hard session: planned tempo, threshold, VO2max, anaerobic, neuromuscular or lactate-clearance work, or strength, SFR, climbing or sprint work.

Checking in normally: on at least half the days of the normal window. Late uploads are not checked: Vekta's API does not give upload times.

Tiers: Act now = three or more layers, or a hard flag. Watching = two layers, or one signal stronger on each of the last three mornings. Logged = one signal. Clear = nothing outside normal.

The private log stays on this computer in log/<athlete>.json. Nothing is written to Vekta unless the Vekta log switch is on.
