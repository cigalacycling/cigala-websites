  1. Who we are

    This website, cigalacycling.com, is run by Cigala Cycling Ltd, a company registered in Ireland (Company Registration No. 675577, VAT No. IE3699893CH) and based in Co. Wicklow ("Cigala Cycling", "we", "us"). We are the controller of the personal data described in this policy.

    For any privacy question or request, contact us at info@cigalacycling.com or +353 404 78107.

    Our other websites each have their own privacy policy covering the services and tools they use. coaching.cigalacycling.com and retail.cigalacycling.com are also run by Cigala Cycling Ltd. tours.cigalacycling.com is run by our sister company, Cigala Cycling Tours Ltd (Company Registration No. 780307). By using this website you confirm that you have read this policy.

  2. What we collect and why

    Contact form: your name, email address, phone number and message. We use them only to answer your enquiry. Legal basis: your request and our legitimate interest in replying to it.

    Newsletter: your email address, if you choose to subscribe. We use it to send you news, blogs, tips, special deals and tour dates. Legal basis: your consent, which you can withdraw at any time.

    Live chat: the messages and any details you choose to share in the chat. We use them to answer you. Legal basis: your request and our legitimate interest in replying to it.

    Website analytics and advertising: information about how you use the site, such as pages visited, device, browser and approximate location, collected only if you accept cookies (see "Cookies" below). Legal basis: your consent.

    Technical data: like every website, our host's servers automatically record basic technical information (such as IP address, browser and the time of your visit) to keep the site running and secure. Legal basis: our legitimate interest in protecting the website.

    Please do not send us health, medical or other sensitive information through the website, contact form or chat. If you need to share such information for coaching or a tour, we will tell you how to do so securely.

    We do not store or maintain credit card details, nor do we share customer financial details with any third parties.

  3. Cookies

    When you first visit, a banner asks whether you accept cookies. Until you choose, and if you reject, we do not set analytics or advertising cookies.

    If you accept, we use:

    • Google Analytics, provided by Google Ireland Limited, to understand how visitors use the site. Google Consent Mode keeps it in a cookie-free state until you accept.
    • Meta Pixel, provided by Meta Platforms Ireland Limited, to measure and improve our Facebook and Instagram advertising.
    • The OpenWidget live chat, provided by Text S.A. (Poland), which loads automatically after you accept. If you have not accepted, the chat only loads when you click "Chat with us".

    We also store your cookie choice and newsletter pop-up status in your browser (local storage), so we don't ask you again.

    You can change your choice at any time with the "Cookie settings" link at the bottom of every page, or by deleting cookies in your browser.

  4. Who receives your data

    We only share personal data with trusted service providers that help us run the site, and only as far as they need it:

    • Hostinger, our website host (EU).
    • Brevo (Sendinblue SAS, France), which stores our newsletter list and sends our emails.
    • Google Ireland Limited (Google Analytics, and Google Fonts, which delivers the website's fonts and receives your IP address when a page loads).
    • Meta Platforms Ireland Limited (Meta Pixel, only if you accept cookies).
    • Text S.A. (OpenWidget live chat).

    These providers act on our instructions or under their own privacy terms, and must protect your data. We may also disclose personal data where the law requires it, for example to a court or public authority, or to protect our rights, our customers or others.

    Some of these providers may transfer data outside the European Economic Area, for example to the United States. Where they do, they use legal safeguards approved by the European Commission, such as the EU-US Data Privacy Framework or Standard Contractual Clauses.

    We never sell your personal data.

  5. How long we keep it

    Contact form and chat enquiries: for as long as needed to deal with your enquiry, and no longer than two years afterwards, unless we need to keep them longer for legal, tax or insurance reasons or to deal with a claim.

    Newsletter: until you unsubscribe. Every email has an unsubscribe link.

    Analytics data: kept in Google Analytics in line with our retention settings, after which it is deleted automatically.

  6. How we protect your data

    The website uses an encrypted connection (HTTPS). Access to our website, email and newsletter accounts is restricted and password-protected, and we only work with reputable providers that apply their own security measures.

    No method of sending or storing data over the internet is completely secure. We take reasonable steps to protect your data, but we cannot guarantee absolute security, and you share information with us at your own risk. If a data breach is likely to affect your rights, we will inform you and the Data Protection Commission as the law requires.

  7. Children

    This website and our newsletter are not intended for children under 16. We do not knowingly collect personal data from children under 16. Coaching or tours for younger riders are arranged directly with a parent or guardian. If you believe a child has sent us personal data, contact us and we will delete it.

  8. Other websites and social media

    This website links to other websites and platforms, including our other Cigala Cycling sites, WhatsApp, Instagram, Facebook, YouTube, TikTok, X and Threads. When you follow those links or contact us on those platforms, their own privacy policies apply. We are not responsible for the content or privacy practices of websites and platforms we do not control.

  9. Your rights

    Under the GDPR you have the right to:

    • access the personal data we hold about you;
    • have it corrected or deleted;
    • object to or restrict how we use it;
    • receive it in a portable format;
    • withdraw your consent at any time (for example by unsubscribing, or through "Cookie settings"). This does not affect anything we did before you withdrew it.

    To use any of these rights, email info@cigalacycling.com. We may need to confirm your identity first. We will reply within one month. We do not make decisions about you based solely on automated processing.

    If you are not happy with how we handle your data, please contact us first so we can try to resolve it. You also have the right to complain to the Data Protection Commission in Ireland (www.dataprotection.ie).

  10. Governing law

    This policy is governed by the laws of Ireland. Nothing in it limits any rights you have under data protection law.

  11. Changes to this policy

    We may update this policy when our website, services or the law change. The latest version is always on this page, and it applies from the date shown below.
