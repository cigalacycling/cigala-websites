Every March, thousands of cyclists travel to Siena to ride one of the world's most iconic cycling events: the Strade Bianche Gran Fondo.

Following many of the same white roads used by the professional race, the event combines smooth Tuscan tarmac, historic villages and the famous strade bianche — the white limestone roads that have become one of the symbols of Italian cycling.

Whether you are planning to ride the Gran Fondo or simply want to experience these legendary roads yourself, this guide covers everything you need to know.

What are the Strade Bianche?

The strade bianche are centuries-old limestone farm roads that connect vineyards, olive groves and medieval villages throughout the Tuscan countryside around Siena.

Unlike rough gravel trails, these roads are generally compact, smooth and perfectly rideable on a modern road bike. Their unique white surface, rolling terrain and incredible scenery make them one of the most beautiful cycling experiences in Europe.

The roads became internationally famous thanks to the professional Strade Bianche race, first held in 2007 and now considered one of the most prestigious one-day races on the cycling calendar.

Can You Ride Strade Bianche on a Road Bike?

Absolutely.

The Strade Bianche Gran Fondo is designed for road cyclists, and the majority of riders complete it on modern road bikes.

The white roads are fast and compact, but the surface can vary depending on weather conditions. A few simple adjustments will make your experience much more enjoyable:

  • 30-32mm tyres are ideal
  • Tubeless tyres are highly recommended
  • Slightly lower tyre pressures improve comfort and grip
  • Compact gearing helps on the steep Tuscan climbs

A gravel bike can also work well, but it is not necessary unless you plan to explore rougher unpaved roads beyond the classic Strade Bianche sectors.

The Strade Bianche Gran Fondo

Held every March in Siena, the Gran Fondo gives amateur cyclists the opportunity to experience many of the same iconic roads used by the professional race.

The event offers:

  • Start and finish in the historic city of Siena
  • Famous white road sectors from the professional race
  • Thousands of cyclists from around the world
  • The incredible atmosphere of race weekend in Tuscany

It is one of Italy's most unique cycling events and a must-do experience for any passionate cyclist.

Is the Strade Bianche Gran Fondo Difficult?

Despite not featuring long Alpine climbs, the Strade Bianche is a challenging event.

The difficulty comes from the combination of:

  • Constant rolling terrain
  • Short and steep climbs
  • Repeated changes between tarmac and white roads
  • Limited opportunities to recover

Tuscany may look gentle on the map, but the accumulated elevation and constant rhythm changes make this a demanding and rewarding challenge.

Best Time to Ride the Strade Bianche

March is the perfect month if you want to experience the Gran Fondo and the incredible atmosphere surrounding the event.

For riders looking for quieter roads, spring and autumn are also fantastic times to explore Tuscany.

The landscape changes throughout the year:

Summer can be very hot, especially in the exposed Tuscan countryside

Spring brings green hills and wildflowers

Autumn offers vineyards full of colour and harvest season

Bike Setup for the Strade Bianche

For the best experience, we recommend:

Bike

  • Modern road bike
  • 30-32mm tyres where possible
  • Tubeless setup preferred

Equipment

  • Two bottles, as some sections are exposed
  • Lightweight jacket or vest for cooler mornings
  • Sunglasses to protect your eyes from dust and small stones

The key is finding the balance between speed on the tarmac and comfort on the white roads.

Frequently Asked Questions

Do I need a gravel bike for Strade Bianche?

No. A modern road bike with wider tyres (around 30-32mm) is perfect for riding the classic Strade Bianche sectors and the Gran Fondo.

Is the Strade Bianche Gran Fondo the same route as the professional race?

The route changes slightly each year, but the Gran Fondo includes many of the iconic white road sectors used by the professional race.

Can beginners ride Strade Bianche?

Yes, but good preparation is recommended. The combination of distance, elevation and changing surfaces makes it a challenging event. There is also the option of the Medio Fondo instead of the Gran Fondo. The Medio Fondo is 80km with approx 1000 mt of elevation.

What tyres are best for Strade Bianche?

For most riders, 30-32mm tubeless road tyres provide the best balance between speed, comfort and confidence.

Should I train specifically for the Strade Bianche Gran Fondo?

Absolutely. While many cyclists can complete the event without following a structured training plan, arriving well prepared will make the experience far more enjoyable.

The repeated climbs, rolling terrain and changing road surfaces make the Strade Bianche more demanding than many riders expect. A personalised training plan can help you improve your climbing, endurance and confidence before race day.
