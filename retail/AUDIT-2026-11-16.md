# Retail shop improvement audit (16 Nov 2026)

This audit was requested by Matteo and relayed by the Coaching session.

**Data used:** the product export (1,787 products, 1,475 live), all 201 collections, 25 pages, the blog, and the 234-page live crawl of 15 Nov.

## Fine already

- **Broken links:** none. All 1,450 internal links found point to live items; links with ™/® in the handle resolve.
- **Year:** no "2026" in SEO titles or descriptions that should say 2027.
- **Duplicate SEO titles and descriptions:** no custom ones are duplicated on products.
- **Social share tags:** og: and twitter: tags are on every page.
  - Products share their own image.
  - Collections, pages and the blog share the team ride photo, not the logo.

## Findings and proposals (need Matteo's OK)

1. **Image alt text:** 3,113 of 6,605 product images (in 602 products) have no alt text.
   - Proposal: set the alt to the product title, adding "– image 2", "– image 3" for extra photos.
   - Existing alt text is kept.
   - Nothing changes visually; it helps Google Images, AI search and screen readers.
2. **Meta descriptions:** 24 live products have no SEO description and almost no description text.
   - These are coaching packages and club kit (Dan Morrissey, Carlow).
   - Proposal: write a short description for each.
3. **Old collection SEO descriptions:** 12 brand and category collections share the same old text ("Cigala Cycling Retail - Ireland's #1 Online Cycling Marketplace for training tools…").
   - Some say "Free shipping to Ireland & UK" or "Free delivery over €50", which is out of date.
   - Proposal: rewrite each one for its brand. (Under CLAUDE.md these metas stay unchanged unless Matteo approves.)
4. **Share preview text on products:** the og/twitter description takes the raw product text, often starting "Free shipping in 1-2 Business Days…", and sometimes shows "&amp;".
   - Proposal: use the same cleaned description as the Google meta description.
   - This is a theme change.
5. **Share image on collections:** every collection shares the same team photo.
   - Proposal: share the collection's own image, or its best-selling product.
   - This is a theme change.
6. **Same-name collections:** these give the same title on different URLs:
   - 4iiii / 4iiii-1, Power Bar / power-bar-1, Quarq / quarq-1, Lightweight / lightweight-1
   - The empty Zipp 302/303/404/454/808/858/Super 9 collections (0 products).
   - The "Shoes" collection has 0 products.
   - Proposal: Matteo decides whether to merge them (with redirects) or rename them. These are URL changes, so nothing happens without his OK.
7. **No image:** one live product, "1x One to One Session" (coaching).
8. **Small main images:** 128 live products have a main image narrower than 600px, for example FSA Powerbox, C-Bear pulleys and Rotor INspider. Fixing these needs better images from the brands or distributors.
