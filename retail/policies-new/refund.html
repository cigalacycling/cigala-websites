Refund Policy

-----

Returns 

  • After your return is processed, you’ll receive a refund within 3 working days on the original payment method used. For more info, read our refund policy below.
  • Sometimes, the Track & Trace information shows we’ve received your return, but you haven’t received an email confirmation yet. We strive to process your return within 48 hours. This excludes Saturdays and Sundays.
  • We cannot accept returned products with visible traces of usage, missing parts or a missing / damaged original packaging. In these cases, we’ll send the product back to you.

Holidays return policy

During the festive season we have an extended returns policy. You now have up until 31 January to return orders placed between 1 and 24 December, so you don't have to worry about being able to return items that aren't exactly what you wanted. You'll receive a refund via the original payment method.

Please note: The extended returns policy only applies to products that are unused and still in their original packaging. Products with a '30 day satisfaction guarantee' label or a 'guaranteed fit' label that have been tested or tried out unfortunately do not fall under the extended returns policy -the normal 30 day return period applies to these products.

Conditions & exceptions
Not sure if your product can be returned? Then view the return conditions and exceptions. Still not quite sure? Contact us.

Conditions:

  • All goods must be unused, unopened, inactivated, in a saleable condition and in their original packaging with all labels still attached.
  • Returning an item is possible up until 30 days from the date mentioned on the purchase invoice;
  • The product is complete, unused, undamaged and in the original packaging;
  • When the return concerns a combined deal or a bundled product, it’s only possible to return the complete combined deal or bundle.

Exceptions which cannot be returned:

  • Items or products with signs of usage or mounting;
  • Products that have been opened or unsealed
  • Items or products with missing or damaged original packaging;
  • Products with software of which the seal has been broken;
  • GPS devices that have been registered or used;
  • Clothing that has been washed or shows signs of use;
  • Products that cannot be returned for health or hygiene reasons;
  • Products that have been registered to your name
  • Products that are clearly personal in nature;
  • Products that spoil or age quickly;
  • Products that are custom made for you, ordered on demand, not in stock and pre-ordered on your request; products or a variant of a product that are not on our site and specially ordered from the manufacturer for you. This includes Pissei custom clothing orders.
  • Products returned in an unsatisfactory condition;

Refunds (if applicable) 
Once your return is received and inspected, we will send you an email to notify you that we have received your returned item. We will also notify you of the approval or rejection of your refund. 
If you are approved, then your refund will be processed, and a credit will automatically be applied to your credit card or original method of payment, within a certain amount of days.

Late or missing refunds (if applicable) 
If you haven’t received a refund yet, first check your bank account again. 
Then contact your credit card company, it may take some time before your refund is officially posted. 
Next contact your bank. There is often some processing time before a refund is posted. 
If you’ve done all of this and you still have not received your refund yet, please contact us at retail@cigalacycling.com.

Exchanges (if applicable) 
We do not exchange products. We want to ensure you receive a product as quickly as possible. To make this possible, you can instead return a product and place a new order for the right product immediately.

You’ll get a refund for the returned item within 3 working days after we’ve received and processed your return.

To exercise this right please contact us prior to the expiry of the cancellation rights period.

However, you are responsible for paying the return delivery charges.

We may make a deduction from the reimbursement for loss in value of any goods supplied, if the loss is the result of unnecessary handling by you.

We will make the reimbursement without undue delay, and not later than:

14 days after the day we received back from you any goods supplied; or
(if earlier) 14 days after the day you provide evidence that you have returned the goods; or

Pre-order policy
When you place a pre-order with us, we contact the appropriate distributor and place the order. If the item is available, we will then get order confirmation which we then confirm with you, along with the ETA. At this point, we have committed to the pre-order and can no longer cancel the item without penalty. Therefore, please be very sure when placing your pre-order as there are cancellation fees incurred if you change your mind. The cancellation fee for a pre-order is equal to 30% of the total amount, which for some orders of large amounts such as bikes, that is the non-refundable deposit that is required when placing the pre-order.

Estimated Time of Arrival (ETA)
ETA's can vary and are not guaranteed arrival date. We list the date given by manufacturer, but delays can and do happen. We cannot cancel your pre-order, even if delays are substantial, so please take this into account when ordering. We regularly update the product listing with the latest ETA. Keep an eye on the listings after you order to see when your product is due to arrive.

Settling a Pre-Order When It Arrives
When your item arrives we will contact you to settle any balance due and arrange shipping.
Orders must be settled within 7 days of the product coming into stock. Failure to settle within this period may mean you lose your pre-ordered item.

Prices quoted can be subject to change as items are only billed by manufacturers when they ship to us. We do everything we can to give you an accurate buy price, but pre-orders that are far away can vary in price if exchange rate varies from now until then. 

Postage fees can also vary depending on the size of the product, type of shipping and number of boxes required. We suggest you pre-order each item individually as that way each pre-order's postage is paid separately and can ship as it comes into stock.
