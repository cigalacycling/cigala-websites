FAQ

Do you have a maximum or a minimum number?

No, we can accommodate as little and as many as you are on our custom trips.

What kind of bike are available to rent?

Road, Gravel, MTB, E-bike, E-Road, Trekking bike.

Once I get the airport, how can I get to the starting point?

Don't worry we will be at the airport. We organise the trip from start to finish.

We don't need a guide, do you also offer self-guided tour?

Yes, we can organise everything else from transfers, hotels and giving you the route details, giving you our restaurants suggestions.

Can you put together a tour in a few different areas?

Yes, of course. We often organise tours that covers a few regions or even countries. Even point A to B.

Your tours on the site look easy/hard. Can I make the stages easier/harder?

Yes. Custom tours are fully tailored. We can adapt each days at your wishes.
