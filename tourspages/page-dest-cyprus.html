Cyprus

A PARADISE FOR CYCLING, ESPECIALLY GRAVEL

Cyprus is a hidden gem for cyclists, offering year-round sunshine, smooth roads, challenging mountain climbs, scenic coastal rides, and gravel paths that make it a paradise for off-road enthusiasts. Our guided Cyprus cycling tours combine performance training with breathtaking landscapes, warm hospitality, and Mediterranean culture.

Good to know

CYPRUS FACTS

Location

Cyprus is the jewel of the Eastern Mediterranean, with routes stretching from the turquoise coastlines around Paphos and Limassol to the rugged Troodos Mountains at the island’s heart. The variety makes it ideal for both endurance training and relaxed cycling escapes.

Climate

Cyprus boasts more than 320 days of sunshine a year, making it one of the best year-round cycling destinations in Europe. Winters are mild and perfect for training camps, while spring and autumn bring warm, dry weather and vibrant landscapes.

Cuisine

The island’s cuisine blends Mediterranean freshness with Middle Eastern influences. Expect grilled meats, fresh fish, halloumi cheese, olives, seasonal vegetables, and local wines. Traditional meze meals are the perfect way to recover after a long ride.

Terrain

Cycling terrain in Cyprus is diverse. Long coastal stretches give way to rolling hills, while the Troodos Mountains offer sustained climbs with gradients to rival classic European routes. Smooth tarmac and light traffic outside main towns make it a dream for road cyclists.
