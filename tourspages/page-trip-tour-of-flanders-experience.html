The trip

RIDE THE COBBLE, LIVE THE CLASSICS

The Tour of Flanders is not just a race — it’s a cultural phenomenon.

Known as one of cycling’s five Monuments, it’s where legends are made on steep cobbled climbs and roaring crowds line every berg. This is your chance to experience it all.

Ride the iconic cobbles yourself in the famous sportive, then witness the pros battle it out the next day on the same roads.

From the Oude Kwaremont to the Paterberg, this is pure cycling history.

Itinerary

DAY TO DAY BREAKDOWN

GPX files will be sent before the trip.

  1. DAY1

    ARRIVAL DAY & THE FLEMISH CLASSICS EXPERIENCE

    60 km

    Welcome to Flanders! After check-in, in the afternoon e'll head out for an easy shake-out ride through the heart of the Flemish Ardennes. We'll tackle some of the region's most iconic climbs, including the Oude Kwaremont and Paterberg, while getting a first taste of the legendary cobbled roads that make the Tour of Flanders so unique. The day finishes with a group dinner and a race briefing.

  2. DAY2

    TOUR OF FLANDERS RECON RIDE

    40 km

    Today's ride is all about confidence and preparation. We'll cover many of the key climbs and sectors featured in the Tour of Flanders, refining pacing, bike handling on the cobbles, tyre pressures and race-day nutrition. The pace will remain comfortable to keep the legs fresh ahead of the sportive.

  3. DAY3

    WE RIDE FLANDERS SPORTIVE

    40 km

    Today is what this whole weekend has been building toward. Based on your chosen distance, you’ll set off into the heart of Flanders and experience the roads, the atmosphere, and the energy of event day. We’ll bring you to the start line in the morning—then it’s your moment. Out on the course, we’ll be there throughout the day, supporting you, cheering you on, and making sure you feel part of something special all the way to the finish.

  4. DAY4

    PRO RACE EXPERIENCE

    The bikes stay still, but the energy only grows.

    We head out together to watch the men’s and women’s pro races unfold on the iconic roads of Flanders.

    Standing roadside, surrounded by fans, you’ll feel the race pass through you—fast, loud, and unforgettable

  5. DAY5

    LEAVING DAY

    The weekend comes to an end, but the memories don’t. After breakfast, it’s time to say goodbye as we begin our journeys home. A chance to reflect on everything we’ve experienced together—long rides, iconic roads, and moments that will stay with you long after Flanders. Until next time..

Good to know

Frequently Asked Questions

Do I need to bring my own bike, or can I rent one?

You're welcome to bring your own bike, or rent one for the week. After booking, you will receive a form to fill out via email. You can let us know there and in the case you want to rent, you will see an option to fill up details for your measurements. 
You can choose a range of bikes: road, gravel or e-road bike.

What fitness level do I need for this trip?

This trip suits Beginner, Intermediate and Expert riders. The core Flanders Experience routes are approachable for most fitness levels — on Sportive day you have the option to choose a longer or a shorter route based on your fitness level.

What kind of support is included on this trip?

A daily support van follows the group throughout the trip, giving you the full pro experience. It carries spares, snacks, drinks, and any spare gear or jackets you want to shed mid-ride.

During the Ride Flanders Sportive, we set up our own dedicated feed zones along the route — just like the pros get. You'll find snacks and drinks stocked and ready, and you can drop off your own bidons or specific nutrition ahead of time so it's waiting for you when you ride through. Our team is on hand at each zone, and there's basic mechanical assistance available too, just in case you need a quick fix to keep going.

Is there a single room supplement?

Yes, a single room supplement is available and you choose it during the booking process.

Is best a road or a gravel bike for this tour?

A road bike with 30-32mm tires is certainly more than enough. You can also do it with 28mm. A gravel bike is not necessary.

How do I sign up for the Sportive?

After booking, you will receive an email with the instructions to sign up to the event.

Should I specifically train for a trip like this?

If you're planning to ride the Ride Flanders Sportive, some endurance and climbing preparation makes a real difference — steep gradients on cobble roads like this reward riders who've built specific climbing endurance rather than just general fitness. If you'd like a structured plan built around this trip, visit our cycling coaching page and Matteo can help you put together a plan based on your current level and the time you have before you travel.

What should I pack for this trip?

Beyond your usual riding kit, bring extra layers, and some rain gear since is early season and you can expect the frequent showers. Our full guide to packing for a European cycling holiday covers everything else you'll need for the week.
