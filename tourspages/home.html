Our story

Ever wondered what a ride built entirely around the road, the people and the moment would feel like? I did too.

Cigala Cycling Tours grew out of years of coaching riders of every level, and the realisation that people don’t just want to ride, they want to experience. So I built tours around exactly that: hand-picked roads, small groups, no crowds, no shortcuts.

Every trip is guided by a team of former pros who know these roads because they’ve ridden them for years, not read them off a script. Good food, real local stops, and the kind of ride you actually remember.

More than a decade in, that idea hasn’t changed. We’d love to welcome you on the road.

Matteo CigalaTours Director

Good to know

Frequently asked questions

What fitness level do I need?

Our tours are graded by difficulty (beginner, advanced and expert), from relaxed coastal routes to high-mountain climbs, so riders of all fitness levels can find a suitable trip.

Do you offer bike rental?

Yes. Road, gravel, MTB and e-bike rentals are available for every destination.

How do airport transfers work?

Airport transfers are arranged at a set time for all public tours; you will find the details in the tour description. We can also arrange a private transfer at a supplement if you arrive at a different time. Just let us know your flight details when booking.

Are support vehicles included?

Every guided tour includes a support vehicle, mechanical assistance, and water and snacks throughout each day’s ride.

What is included in the price?

Each tour page lists exactly what is included. As an example, the Dolomites tour includes hotel nights, buffet breakfasts, airport transfers with bike and luggage at set times, guided rides every day with our ex-pro guides, a support vehicle, snacks and water on the route, and GPX files sent in advance. Flights, dinners, bike rental and travel insurance are extra.

How big are the groups?

We keep groups small so everyone gets proper support. The Dolomites tour, for example, takes a maximum of 12 riders. You will find the group size on each tour page.

How do I book, and when is the balance due?

A deposit secures your place when you book. Deposits are non-refundable. The outstanding balance is due 31 days before the trip.

What happens if I need to cancel?

Email us at tours@cigalacycling.com. If you cancel between booking and 31 days before departure, your full balance (not including the deposit) is carried forward as a credit for a future trip within two years. Cancellations fewer than 31 days before the trip cannot be refunded, which is why travel insurance is mandatory on all our tours.

Can you organise a private or custom tour?

Yes. We are a boutique tour operator and specialise in bespoke tours for any group size, from a couple of riders to a whole club. We organise accommodation, guiding, support, bike rental, transfers and restaurants, and we can also put together a self-guided trip.
