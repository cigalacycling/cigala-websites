The trip

EXPLORE THE HIDDEN SIDE OF THE ITALIAN ALPS

Experience the Italian Alps from a different perspective on an unforgettable gravel cycling adventure. This unique tour combines legendary climbs, spectacular alpine scenery and incredible gravel routes, taking you beyond the traditional roads and into the heart of the mountains.

Ride the iconic Stelvio Gravel, explore historic military roads, remote alpine trails and hidden valleys while discovering some of the most breathtaking landscapes in Northern Italy. From challenging climbs to flowing gravel descents, every day offers a new adventure and a true sense of achievement.

This is the perfect cycling holiday for riders looking to experience the beauty of the Italian Alps in a different way — combining epic terrain, adventure and the freedom of gravel riding.

Itinerary

DAY TO DAY BREAKDOWN

GPX files will be sent before the trip.

  1. DAY1

    ARRIVAL DAY

    After arrival, enjoy the drive to Bormio and spend the afternoon in the beautiful town.
    Adjust your bike and feel free to do a quick spin to test it before the days ahead.
    A great meal is waiting for you after testing the bike.

  2. DAY2

    STELVIO GRAVEL ADVENTURE

    60 km2,500 m

    The highlight of the trip. Today we take on one of the most iconic climbs in cycling history from a completely different perspective: the Stelvio Gravel. Ride beyond the famous asphalt hairpins and discover incredible off-road routes with breathtaking views of the surrounding mountains.

    A truly unforgettable alpine gravel experience.

  3. DAY3

    LIVIGNO GRAVEL

    70 km2,000 m

    A true alpine gravel classic. Ride through the spectacular landscapes of the Stelvio National Park, climbing towards Lago di Cancano before crossing towards Livigno via Passo Alpisella and remote military gravel roads.

    A highlight of the week with incredible views, high-altitude riding and some of the best gravel terrain in the Alps.

  4. DAY4

    VAL VIOLA

    75 km1,900 m

    The queen stage of the trip. Ride deep into Val Viola, one of the most beautiful valleys in the Alps, featuring remote gravel climbs, turquoise alpine lakes, mountain huts and endless panoramic views.

    A challenging but unforgettable day in a truly wild alpine environment.

  5. DAY5

    VAL GROSINA

    75 km1,600 m

    Another epic day on the gravel bike. We descent down to Grosio via a cycle path and then turn right in Grosio into the Val Grosina, which offer beautiful gravel and stunning views of the Valtellina. Finish with a scenic return to Bormio, legs heavy but spirits high.

  6. DAY6

    CANCANO LAKES

    40 km1,200 m

    After breakfast, we set off for a rewarding morning ride to the Lago di Cancano, via road and returning via the gravel paths. 
    After the ride, we head to the airport for your return flight. See you next time! :-)

Frequently Asked Questions

Do I need to bring my own bike, or can I rent one?

You're welcome to bring your own bike, or rent one for the week. After booking, you will receive a form to fill out via email. You can let us know there and in the case you want to rent, you will see an option to fill up details for your measurements. 

What fitness level do I need for this trip?

This trip is suited to Expert riders. You should have a high level of fitness, endurance, and technical cycling ability appropriate for the demands of the tour.

Are support vehicles included?

No, support vehicles are not included on this Alpine gravel tour, as vans cannot access many of the remote Alpine gravel trails. However, your guide will carry essential spare parts and tools, and you should carry enough water, snacks and personal gear for each ride.

Is there a single room supplement?

Yes, a single room supplement is available. Within 24–48 hours of booking, you’ll receive a confirmation email containing a form to complete. Simply let us know your preference there, and we’ll arrange the single room supplement for you.

Do I have to ride the full Stelvio climb, or can I skip that day?

No, you don’t have to ride every day. While the itinerary is designed to be enjoyed as a complete cycling experience, you’re welcome to skip a ride or take a rest day if needed. Just let your guide know in advance so we can help with the arrangements

I see you have a road tour running at the same time, can I join one or two road rides?

Yes. Our Italian Alps road tour runs at the same time, and both groups share the same accommodation and evening restaurants. You can swap in a road day or two: just let us know when booking and we will rent a road bike for you for the day.

Should I specifically train for a trip like this?

If you're planning to ride the Stelvio day, some dedicated climbing preparation makes a real difference — sustained gradients like this reward riders who've built specific climbing endurance rather than just general fitness. If you'd like a structured plan built around this trip, visit our cycling coaching page and Matteo can help you put together a plan based on your current level and the time you have before you travel.

What should I pack for this trip?

 Beyond your usual riding kit, bring layers for the Stelvio day — temperatures at the summit can be significantly cooler than down in the valley, even in summer. Our full guide to packing for a European cycling holiday covers everything else you'll need for the week.
