The trip

RIDE IN LAKE BLED, THE SOČA RIVER & VRŠIČ PASS

A spectacular road cycling holiday through Slovenia, taking in some of the country's most beautiful roads and landscapes.

Starting and finishing in Nova Gorica, this six-day journey climbs through the Julian Alps, skirts the turquoise waters of the Soča River, passes the iconic Lake Bled, and tackles the legendary Vršič Pass — one of the most scenic climbs in Europe.

Beyond the riding, expect charming medieval towns, incredible hilltop churches, alpine scenery, and warm Slovenian hospitality along the way. 

This trip is designed for cyclists who want a genuine road cycling challenge combined with some of the most breathtaking scenery the Alps have to offer — a true bucket-list adventure for anyone who loves this sport.

Itinerary

DAY TO DAY BREAKDOWN

GPX files will be sent before the trip.

  1. DAY1

    ARRIVAL DAY

    Welcome to Slovenia! After a good dinner in Italy, we arrive at the hotel and check in. It's an early night tonight, resting up and getting ready for the first day of cycling tomorrow.

    Hotel: Klanjšček Wine & Stay *** – Nova Gorica

  2. DAY2

    NOVA GORICA → POSTOJNA

    85 km1,500 m

    Today the real riding begins. We leave Nova Gorica behind and head into the Slovenian countryside, tackling a solid amount of climbing as the roads wind through vineyards, farmland, and small villages that see very little traffic. It's a proper introduction to the terrain — testing early on, but with plenty of scenery to take your mind off the effort. We finish the day in Postojna, home to one of the most famous karst cave systems in the world, a great base to rest up before tomorrow.

    Hotel: Hotel Jama **** – Postojna

  3. DAY3

    POSTOJNA → ŠKOFJA LOKA

    83 km900 m

    Leaving Postojna, the route heads north through classic Slovenian landscapes — rolling hills, dense forest, and glimpses of the Alps beginning to rise on the horizon. The climbing today is steadier and more manageable, giving legs a chance to recover ahead of the bigger days to come. We roll into Škofja Loka in the afternoon, a beautifully preserved medieval town with a hilltop castle and cobbled streets — one of the prettiest overnight stops of the whole trip.

    Hotel: Turizem Loka **** – Škofja Loka

  4. DAY4

    ŠKOFJA LOKA → LAKE BLED

    107 km1,900 m

    The longest and toughest day of the tour. We climb steadily out of Škofja Loka into properly alpine terrain, with long ascents and rewarding descents through some of the most dramatic scenery in Slovenia. It's a day that demands respect, but one that pays off in full: as the kilometres tick by and the legs get heavier, the reward at the end is one of the most photographed views in Europe — Lake Bled, with its island church and clifftop castle reflected in impossibly turquoise water. A well-earned dinner and a good night's sleep await.

    Hotel: Old Bled House *** – Lake Bled

  5. DAY5

    LAKE BLED → BOVEC

    80 km1,600 m

    Today is the highlight of the trip. We leave Lake Bled and head deep into the Julian Alps, climbing towards the legendary Vršič Pass — Slovenia's highest mountain pass and one of the most spectacular roads in the country, with 50 hairpin turns cut into the mountainside and views that stretch across jagged alpine peaks in every direction. From the summit, a thrilling descent drops us down into the Trenta Valley and alongside the Soča River, famous for its striking emerald-green water. We follow the river valley into Bovec, surrounded by towering peaks, ending a day that will stay with you long after the trip is over.

    Hotel: Hotel Mangart **** – Bovec

  6. DAY6

    BOVEC → NOVA GORICA

    85 km700 m

    The final stage of the tour, and a fitting send-off. We continue to trace the Soča River as it winds south, its colour shifting through every shade of blue and green as the valley opens up around us. The climbing eases off today, giving everyone a chance to soak in the scenery and reflect on the week behind them. We finish back where we started, in Nova Gorica, with a real sense of achievement — six days, hundreds of kilometres, and some of the best roads Slovenia has to offer.

    Accommodation: Klanjšček Wine & Stay *** – Nova Gorica

  7. DAY7

    LEAVING DAY

    After breakfast, airport transfer to Venice Airport. ​See you next time 🙂

Frequently Asked Questions

Do I need to bring my own bike, or can I rent one?

You're welcome to bring your own bike, or rent one for the week. After booking, you will receive a form to fill out via email. You can let us know there and in the case you want to rent, you will see an option to fill up details for your measurements. 
You can choose a range of bikes: road or e-road bike.

What fitness level do I need for this trip?

This trip suits Beginner, Intermediate and Expert riders. Most of the days are achievable for all levels and the longest and most challenging day, we have an option for a short cut.

What kind of support is included on this trip?

A daily support van follows the group throughout the trip, giving you the full pro experience. It carries spares, snacks, drinks, and any spare gear or jackets you want to shed mid-ride.
Additionally the van will move your suitcases from one hotel to the other.
Finally, our guides are expert mechanics, so you can rely for any adjustments, punctures etc.

Is there a single room supplement?

Yes, a single room supplement is available and you choose it during the booking process.

If I bring my bike, what would happen to the bike box since we change hotel each day?

The hotel on the first day and last day is the same. We would therefore store the bike box there so that you have it again before leaving on the last day. 

What would be the weather in September?

September is one of the best months to visit Slovenia. 
Temperatures are between 20-30 degrees Celsius each day, making it ideal for cycling.

Should I specifically train for a trip like this?

If you want to enjoy the climbs and arrive each evening a little fresher, some training beforehand is recommended. If you'd like a structured plan built around this trip, visit our cycling coaching page and Matteo can help you put together a plan based on your current level and the time you have before you travel.

What should I pack for this trip?

Beyond your usual riding kit, bring extra layers, and some rain gear just in case you meet the odd shower. Our full guide to packing for a European cycling holiday covers everything else you'll need for the week.
