The trip

STUNNING VIEWS AND FAMOUS GRANFONDO MONUMENT 'IL LOMBARDIA'.

Everybody that is into cycling knows the last monument of the year, "Il Lombardia".
We designed a unique Cycling Holiday basing us on a central location for you to discover the beauty of Lake Como, watch the pros in the last monument of the year and ride the Gran Fondo "Il Lombardia" the next day.
A unique route in an extraordinary landscape. 109,3 km and 2,300 m in vertical altitude gain.
The perfect combination of ascents, descents and steep spurts that ensures fun and enjoyment to all participants.


Your race entry is secured through the OFFICIAL TOUR OPERATOR of Gran Fondo Il Lombardia.

This mean that by booking this trip you will have GUARANTEED ENTRY to the event.

Frequently Asked Questions

Do I need to bring my own bike, or can I rent one?

You're welcome to bring your own bike, or rent one for the week. After booking, you will receive a form to fill out via email. You can let us know there and in the case you want to rent, you will see an option to fill up details for your measurements. 

What fitness level do I need for this trip?

This trip suits Beginner, Intermediate and Expert riders. The core Lake Como routes are approachable for most fitness levels — the Gran Fondo is the only day that demands solid fitness, however is optional.

Are support vehicles included?

Yes every day the support vehicles will be there with snacks and water on route. You can also leave spare layers inside for the descents and anything you might need.

Is there a single room supplement?

Yes, there is the option for you to take a single room. Once you have booked within 48 hrs you will receive a form to complete and you can let us know your room preference.

Do I have to ride Gran Fondo, or can I skip that day?

The Gran Fondo is a highlight for many guests, but it's not compulsory — if you'd rather have a rest day or ride a shorter route around the Lake instead, just let your guide know.

How long are the climbs around Lake Como?

Climbs such as the Ghisallo or Sormano are between 6 and 8km

Should I specifically train for a trip like this?

If you're planning to ride the Gran Fondo, some dedicated climbing preparation makes a real difference — sustained climbs like this reward riders who've built specific endurance rather than just general fitness. If you'd like a structured plan built around this trip, visit our cycling coaching page and Matteo can help you put together a plan based on your current level and the time you have before you travel.

What should I pack for this trip?

Beyond your usual riding kit, bring layers as the trip is held in October. Make sure you also pack your rain gear. You might not need it, however best to have it in the van, just in case. Our full guide to packing for a European cycling holiday covers everything else you'll need for the week.
