Where to ride

THE ULTIMATE WINTER TRAINING DESTINATION

Gran Canaria is a dream destination for cyclists looking for challenging climbs, warm winter weather and incredible variety. From sea level to almost 2,000m, the island offers everything from fast coastal roads to long mountain ascents, with spectacular scenery around every corner. It's no surprise that WorldTour teams return here year after year for their winter training camps. Add great Canarian food, quiet roads and a landscape that feels like a continent in miniature, and Gran Canaria delivers an unforgettable cycling experience.

Gran Canaria's cycling appeal comes down to its extraordinary vertical range — in a single ride you can climb from Atlantic coastline through banana plantations and pine forests to the volcanic, near-desert peaks of the island's interior. Combined with a mild, sunny climate almost all year round, it's exactly why so many professional teams choose the island for their winter and pre-season training camps.

Gran Canaria is one of Spain's Canary Islands, sitting in the Atlantic off the coast of northwest Africa, and its landscape is often described as a miniature continent for good reason. The climb to Pico de las Nieves, the island's highest point, passes through an astonishing variety of microclimates and terrain, while roads around Mogán and the island's south offer gentler, sun-soaked riding. Given its combination of serious climbing and dependable winter climate, Gran Canaria is a destination where visitors can enjoy a holiday full of high-quality training, relaxation and the thrill of riding the same roads as the world's best.

When you think of Canarian food, expect simple, hearty island cuisine with a distinct identity of its own. Papas arrugadas (wrinkled potatoes) served with mojo sauce are the island's signature dish, alongside fresh Atlantic seafood, local goat cheese, and gofio, a toasted grain flour used across generations of Canarian cooking. Canarian wines, grown in distinctive volcanic soils, pair perfectly with a day spent in the saddle. There are countless local restaurants throughout the island, set in wonderful coastal and mountain locations, and we will bring you to the best.

Good to know

GRAN CANARIA FACTS

Location

Gran Canaria is one of Spain's Canary Islands, sitting in the Atlantic Ocean off the northwest coast of Africa. Its terrain rises from sea level to over 1,900m at Pico de las Nieves near the island's centre, packing an extraordinary range of landscapes into a compact area.

Climate

Gran Canaria enjoys a near-constant spring-like climate year-round, with average temperatures rarely dropping below 18-20°C even in winter — a major reason it has become one of the pro peloton's favourite winter and pre-season training destinations.

Cuisine

Canarian cuisine has a distinct island identity, built around papas arrugadas with mojo sauce, fresh Atlantic seafood, local goat cheese and gofio, a traditional toasted grain flour used in both savoury and sweet dishes.

Terrain

Gran Canaria is often called a miniature continent for cyclists, with roads climbing from sea level through banana plantations, pine forests and volcanic highlands to Pico de las Nieves. This range of terrain and altitude within a single island is virtually unmatched in Europe.
