Good to know

IRELAND FACTS

Location

Located on the western edge of Europe, Ireland is known for its dramatic coastlines, rolling green landscapes, charming villages, and rich cycling heritage. From the rugged Wild Atlantic Way to the scenic Wicklow Mountains, Ireland offers some of the most beautiful and peaceful roads in Europe.​

Climate

reland enjoys a mild maritime climate, making it an excellent cycling destination from spring through autumn. Temperatures are generally comfortable for riding, with cool mornings, pleasant afternoons, and lush green landscapes throughout the season. While the weather can be changeable, it is all part of the authentic Irish experience.

Cuisine

Irish cuisine has evolved dramatically in recent years, combining traditional recipes with high-quality local ingredients. Expect fresh seafood, artisan cheeses, hearty breakfasts, homemade breads, and world-famous pub meals. Ireland's café culture has also grown significantly, making it a fantastic destination for cyclists who appreciate great coffee and local food.

Terrain

Ireland offers a diverse mix of terrain suitable for all types of cyclists. Expect rolling countryside roads, coastal routes with stunning ocean views, short punchy climbs, and longer mountain ascents in areas such as Wicklow, Kerry, and Connemara. The roads are generally quiet, scenic, and ideal for both endurance riding and adventure cycling.​
