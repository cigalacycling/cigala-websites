THE ALGARVE, EUROPE'S SOUTHERN GEM

Undiscovered and unique, this is the Algarve.

Quiet roads, smooth tarmac, and a stunning coastline define the riding here, creating a region that feels both relaxed and inspiring on the bike. This cycling holiday is perfectly timed as a pre-season trip, ideal for building form ahead of the spring and summer, whether you are a racer or simply looking to enjoy quality riding in great conditions.

The week is built around the Volta ao Algarve, giving you the chance to experience the atmosphere of a pro race and watch the peloton up close on selected stages. The highlight of the trip is the Algarve Gran Fondo on Saturday, followed by the hill climb on Sunday—your opportunity to take part and test yourself on the same roads.

As the official event partner of the Algarve Gran Fondo, we offer a fully integrated experience both on and off the bike.
By booking the trip your entry is guaranteed.

Itinerary

DAY TO DAY BREAKDOWN

GPX files will be sent before the trip.

  1. DAY1

    ARRIVAL DAY AND COFFEE RIDE

    50 km600 m

    Welcome to the Algarve.
    After grabbing some lunch and getting the bike ready, we head out for a short 50 km ride to Sao Bras de Alportel to our favourite coffee stop in the Algarve. A perfect way to start your trip! 

  2. DAY2

    ALTO MALHAO & GRAN FONDO RECCE

    110 km1,900 m

    Today we get a chance to test out the legs and route of the Gran Fondo.
    We climb Malhao which is one of the most famous climbs in the region. 
    On the way back to the hotel, we watch the pro race going by in Alte, by the famous cycling cafe' Germano.

  3. DAY3

    PRE GRAN FONDO RIDE TO QUERENÇA

    40 km350 m

    Today's ride takes you to Querenca, a stunning village located in the heart of the Algarve where you can enjoy a nice cafe stop with some cakes in the town square. The road to Querenca is rolling and very quiet, passing through the Barranco Forest. A great pre Gran Fondo ride.

  4. DAY4

    GRAN FONDO ALGARVE

    130 km2,200 m

    Today is the big day! Is your day to enjoy the biggest cycling event of the year in the Algarve. 
    After the Gran Fondo we get another chance to watch the pros going through the same finish a few our after the amateurs.

  5. DAY5

    TAVIRA RIDE

    65 km750 m
    On today's ride, we head towards Spain into the hills and descent down to Tavira, the last big town before the border. 
    After having lunch in town we return by the coastal road to the hotel.
  6. DAY6

    LEAVING DAY AND COFFEE RIDE

    35 km200 m
    Last day. If feel to do the last quick ride before catching your fly, we have planned a stunning ride around the golf courses of Quinta Do Lago for you. See you next time on another Cycling Holiday!

Good to know

Frequently Asked Questions

Do I need to bring my own bike, or can I rent one?

You're welcome to bring your own bike, or rent one for the week. After booking, you will receive a form to fill out via email. You can let us know there and in the case you want to rent, you will see an option to fill up details for your measurements. 

What fitness level do I need for this trip?

This trip suits any level of cyclist. You'll ride in a group with others of a similar pace, so there's no need to worry about being left behind — everyone waits for each other, and a support vehicle follows the group every day.

Are support vehicles included?

Yes — a support vehicle follows the group every day, carrying spares, snacks, drinks, and any spare gear you want to shed mid-ride.

Is there a single room supplement?

Yes, a single room supplement applies if you want a private room in a shared two bedroom apartment. There is also the option to have your own apartment.

Is there an option also for a E-Road Bike?

Absolutely. Once you book, please let us know the preference and we can give you some options. 

If I sign up to the tour, would I have a guaranteed entry to the Gran Fondo?

Yes by signing up with us, your entry to the Gran Fondo is guaranteed. And not only that, your registration will be automatically upgraded to VIP front starting grid as we are a partner of the event.

Should I specifically train for a trip like this?

If planning to ride the Gran Fondo, and be serious about it, some dedicated preparation makes a real difference.  If you'd like a structured plan built around this trip, visit our cycling coaching page and Matteo can help you put together a plan based on your current level and the time you have before you travel.

What should I pack for this trip?

Beyond your usual riding kit, bring layers and a rain jacket just in case there is the odd shower. Our full guide to packing for a European cycling holiday covers everything else you'll need for the week.
