LAKE GARDA'S QUIETER SIDE, ON GRAVEL

Yes, a paradise — just not the one most cyclists know. There is no better place than Lake Garda for cyclists looking to trade tarmac for gravel. Based in Moniga del Garda, this trip trades the busy lakeside roads for quiet vineyard tracks, glacial hills and wine country most visitors never see.

During this trip, you will experience gravel cycling in one of Europe's top destinations while fully immersing yourself in authentic Italian culture and cuisine — including two of the region's finest wine areas.

Every day is unique — rolling through the vineyard tracks of Valtenesi, the glacial moraine hills of the Colline Moreniche, the flat river paths of the Mincio Valley to Borghetto sul Mincio, and the Amarone wine country of Valpolicella. The moderate elevation and mixed surface profile make it approachable for gravel riders of most levels, not just experts.

This Lake Garda gravel experience is sure to exceed your expectations. It's the perfect blend of adventure, wine country and quiet, undiscovered roads — a cycling holiday you'll remember forever.

Itinerary

DAY TO DAY BREAKDOWN

GPX files will be sent before the trip.

  1. DAY1

    VALTENESI SHAKEDOWN

    35km | 350m elevation gain | 60% gravel After arrival and once everybody's bikes are set up, we head out for an easy loop through San Felice del Benaco, Manerba del Garda and Puegnago — a legs-loosener through home turf, olive groves and lakeside gravel paths to shake off travel before the week begins.
  2. DAY2

    COLLINE MORENICHE DEL GARDA

    75km | 900m | 65% gravel

    The longest day radiating from base. We ride south through Padenghe and Lonato into the glacial moraine hills — Solferino, Castiglione delle Stiviere, Cavriana — historic battlefield terrain, now rolling farm gravel through vineyards and cypress-lined lanes.

  3. DAY3

    BORGHETTO SUL MINCIO & THE MINCIO VALLEY

    80km | 400m | 65% gravel

    Today's route heads south via Desenzano and Lonato onto the Mincio river gravel path — one of the flattest, most scenic tracks in the region — into Borghetto sul Mincio, a borgo built over the river itself with its historic mill wheels, and neighbouring Valeggio sul Mincio, before looping back north to Moniga.

  4. DAY4

    VALPOLICELLA

    60km | 950m | 70% gravel

    A short van transfer (~50 minutes) takes us to Sant'Ambrogio di Valpolicella, where we ride Amarone vineyard tracks through Fumane Valley and Negrar, reaching into the Lessinia foothills, before the van returns everyone to Moniga for the night.

  5. DAY5

    DEEP VALTENESI & VINEYARDS

    55km | 650m | 70% gravel

    Today we head into the heart of Valtenesi wine country — Polpenazze, Soiano del Lago and Puegnago del Garda — riding rolling vineyard tracks and terraces, with a stop for lunch among the vines.

  6. DAY6

    FAREWELL RIDE: SIRMIONE PENINSULA

    40km | 300m | 45% gravel

    On the last day, before catching the flight back, we head along the shore toward Sirmione's peninsula views — a low-key send-off rather than another hard effort, with a stop for a final coffee and lunch on the waterfront.

RELAIS ROSA DEI VENTI

Located on a wide green area, and surrounded by big olive trees. It is just 200 metres from the shores of Lake Garda, and each apartment has a lake-view patio or balcony. The resort is made up of numerous self-catering apartments, located in different buildings. Common facilities include an outdoor swimming pool, a garden with children's playground. Parking is free. Every apartment has a comfortable living room, and a kitchen. All accommodation comes with air conditioning, a satellite TV with DVD player, and a private bathroom with bath or shower. The new tourist harbour and the beach, are about a 2-minute walk away. The town's historic centre is a 5-minute walk.

Frequently Asked Questions

Do I need to bring my own bike, or can I rent one?

You're welcome to bring your own bike, or rent one for the week. After booking, you will receive a form to fill out via email. You can let us know there and in the case you want to rent, you will see an option to fill up details for your measurements. 

What fitness level do I need for this trip?

This trip suits Intermediate and Expert riders. You should have not only appropriate fitness level but also good bike handling skills to join the Lake Garda Gravel Tour.

Are support vehicles included?

On the Lake Gravel Garda tour, we offer the van only as a transfer for the Valpolicella day, where will bring the group to the Valpolicella area and back to Lake Garda.

Is there a single room supplement?

Yes, a single room supplement applies if you want a private room in a shared two bedroom apartment. There is also the option to have your own apartment.

Is there an option also for a E-Gravel Bike?

Absolutely. Once you book, please let us know the preference and we can give you some options. 

I see you have a road tour running at the same time, can I join one or two road rides?

Yes our Lake Garda & Stelvio will be running at the same time. And of course, you can join also join any of the road rides, just let your guide during the trip. If you need to rent a road bike for the day, we can organise it for you, or you can simply use the gravel bike. Just let us know when booking. 

Should I specifically train for a trip like this?

Some dedicated preparation makes a real difference to enjoy fully this trip. The fitness level required for the Lake Garda Gravel tour is intermediate / expert. If you'd like a structured plan built around this trip, visit our cycling coaching page and Matteo can help you put together a plan based on your current level and the time you have before you travel.

What should I pack for this trip?

Beyond your usual riding kit, bring layers and a rain jacket just in case there is the odd shower. Our full guide to packing for a European cycling holiday covers everything else you'll need for the week.
