Croatia

CRYSTAL CLEAR WATER & UNPARALLEL COSTAL BEAUTY

Croatia offers a stunning blend of coastal beauty, historic towns, and rugged inland terrain—all perfect for cyclists. Ride along azure Adriatic waters, through charming fishing villages, vineyards, and pine‐forested hillsides. Combine island hopping with mainland climbs, savor coastal cuisine, relax on secluded beaches, and soak up Croatia’s rich cultural heritage. A tour here is as much about the scenery and coast as about solid riding.

Good to know

CROATIA FACTS

Location

Based in the Dalmatian region, Croatia boasts a dramatic coastline dotted with islands (Hvar, Korčula, Brač, etc.), peninsulas (Pelješac), and coastal towns like Split and Dubrovnik. Inland, you’ll encounter karst plateaus, pine forests, and rolling hills.​

Climate

The climate is Mediterranean — hot, dry summers and mild, wetter winters. On the coast, summer daytime highs typically reach high 20s to low 30s °C. Autumn and spring are milder and ideal for riding. Inland in elevated areas, nights can be cooler.​

Cuisine

Croatian cuisine is a fusion of Mediterranean and Balkan influences. Expect fresh seafood (octopus, grilled fish, shellfish), olive oil, herbs, local cheeses, cured meats, and seasonal vegetables. On the islands, try local wines, olive oils, and fresh dishes in konobas (traditional taverns)

Terrain

Cycling terrain is varied: Coastal roads that roll gently above the sea, with occasional steep climbs to viewpoints or ridgelines Island loops with undulating hills Karst plateaus and inland roads with occasional rugged, stony surfaces Some ferry or boat transfers between islands may be part of the route​
