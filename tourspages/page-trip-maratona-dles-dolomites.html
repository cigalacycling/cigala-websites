Itinerary

DAY TO DAY BREAKDOWN

GPX files will be sent before the trip.

  1. DAY1

    ARRIVAL DAY AND SHAKE DOWN RIDE

    25 km600 m

    Arrive in Alta in Badia, check into your hotel and get set up with your bike fit. In the afternoon, we ease into the mountains with a shakeout ride up Passo Valparola — steady, scenic, and just enough to shake off the travel and get a first proper look at where you'll be racing in a few days' time. Welcome dinner in the evening to meet your guide team and fellow riders.

  2. DAY2

    THE SELLARONDA

    65 km1,800 m

    The big one before the big one. Today we ride the full Sellaronda loop, circling the Sella massif over four legendary passes: Campolongo, Pordoi, Sella and Gardena. It's one of the most iconic rides in cycling — closed roads, endless switchbacks, and some of the finest mountain scenery anywhere in the Alps. Lunch trailside with the peaks in full view, back to Corvara by evening.

  3. DAY3

    GARDENA & CAMPOLONGO

    50 km1,000 m

    Two of the Maratona's classic passes in one day. We climb Passo Gardena first, winding up through pine forest into open alpine meadow with the Sella towers rising ahead, then drop back toward Corvara before taking on Passo Campolongo — shorter, sharper, and a proper leg-opener for what's still to come this week. Lunch trailside, back to the hotel by early afternoon.

  4. DAY4

    REST DAY OR COFFEE RIDE

    30 km200 m

    A day off the bike to recover before race day. Explore Alta Badia, relax at the hotel, or join an easy optional spin if you're restless. Race briefing in the evening before a carbo loading meal ahead of the big one the next day.

  5. DAY5

    MARATONA DLES DOLOMITES

    55 / 106 / 138 km
    Choose your course: Sellaronda 55 km, 1,780 mMedio 106 km, 3,130 mMaratona 138 km, 4,230 m

    This is what you came for. Start line in Corvara alongside thousands of riders, then the full Maratona course — Campolongo, Pordoi, Sella, Gardena, Giau, Falzarego, Valparola — seven passes, closed to traffic, all for you. Our guide team will be on course and at the finish to see you home. Celebration dinner in the evening.

  6. DAY6

    LEAVING DAY

    After an unforgettable week of riding, exploring, and experiencing the magic of the Dolomites, it’s time to say arrivederci.

    Whether you’re heading home or continuing your travels, we hope you leave with tired legs, great memories, and a deeper love for cycling.

    Grazie mille for being part of the journey—and we hope to ride with you again soon!

Good to know

Frequently Asked Questions

Do I need to bring my own bike, or can I rent one?

You're welcome to bring your own bike, or rent one for the week. After booking, you will receive a form to fill out via email. You can let us know there and in the case you want to rent, you will see an option to fill up details for your measurements. 
You can choose a range of bikes: road, gravel or e-road bike.

What fitness level do I need for this trip?

This trip suits Beginner, Intermediate and Expert riders. The core Maratona dles Dolomites routes are approachable for most fitness levels — during the day of the event you have the option to choose a longer or a shorter route based on your fitness level.

What kind of support is included on this trip?

A daily support van follows the group throughout the trip, giving you the full pro experience. It carries spares, snacks, drinks, and any spare gear or jackets you want to shed mid-ride.

During the Maratona dles Dolomites, we set up our own dedicated feed zones along the route — just like the pros get. You'll find snacks and drinks stocked and ready, and you can drop off your own bidons or specific nutrition ahead of time so it's waiting for you when you ride through. Our team is on hand at each zone, and there's basic mechanical assistance available too, just in case you need a quick fix to keep going.

Is there a single room supplement?

Yes, a single room supplement is available and you choose it during the booking process.

Once I book the trip is the entry to the Gran Fondo guaranteed?

Yes we are Official Tour Operator of Maratona dles Dolomites.
When booking the package with us, your entry is included. You will be starting in 3rd grid. According to the conditions of entry, women will automatically be placed in the 2nd starting group.
However, women have the possibility to start together with their friends/companions from the 3rd group.
In general, it is possible to start in a later starting group, but it is not possible to start in an earlier group than the one assigned.

Can I stay shorter than 5 days?

The minimum stay is 4 days. If you are planning to stay less than 5 days and missing the airport transfers on the Wednesday, you can make your own way to accommodation or organise a private airport transfer at a supplement.

Should I specifically train for a trip like this?

If you're planning to ride the Medium or the Long route, some endurance and climbing preparation makes a real difference — long climbs and elevation gain reward riders who've built specific climbing endurance rather than just general fitness. If you'd like a structured plan built around this trip, visit our cycling coaching page and Matteo can help you put together a plan based on your current level and the time you have before you travel.

Read also Matteo's guide about training for the Maratona dles Dolomites. 

What should I pack for this trip?

Beyond your usual riding kit, bring extra layers and some rain gear since you are in the mountains and you can expect the occasional shower. Our full guide to packing for a European cycling holiday covers everything else you'll need for the week.
