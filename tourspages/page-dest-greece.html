Greece

RIDE THROUGH ANCIENT GREECE

Greece offers the perfect combination of ancient history, spectacular mountains and stunning Mediterranean scenery. From the rugged Peloponnese to the wild landscapes of Crete, discover quiet roads, challenging climbs, historic ruins and crystal-clear coastlines. Add exceptional food and authentic Greek hospitality, and you have a cycling destination that remains wonderfully off the beaten track. Our aim is simple: the best routes, the best experiences and an unforgettable cycling holiday.

Greece's cycling appeal lies in its extraordinary combination of history and terrain. Quiet mountain roads climb past ancient ruins and Byzantine monasteries, while the country's islands each offer their own distinct character — from Crete's dramatic White Mountains to the gentler, whitewashed hills of the Cyclades.

Greece sits at the southeastern edge of Europe, where the Mediterranean, the Aegean and thousands of years of history meet. The Peloponnese offers some of mainland Greece's best cycling, with mountain roads connecting ancient sites like Olympia and Mystras, while Crete's Lefka Ori (White Mountains) provide genuine high-mountain climbing with the Mediterranean visible from the summit. Given its combination of rich history and varied terrain, Greece is a destination where visitors can enjoy a holiday full of discovery, serious riding and the thrill of exploring a country still relatively new to cycling tourism.

When you think of Greek food, expect fresh, simple ingredients built around one of the world's healthiest culinary traditions. Moussaka, souvlaki and fresh Greek salads sit alongside exceptional olive oil, feta and other regional cheeses, and a seafood tradition shaped by thousands of kilometres of coastline. Greek wines, particularly the crisp Assyrtiko from volcanic Santorini, pair perfectly with a day spent in the saddle. There are countless tavernas throughout the country, set in wonderful coastal and mountain locations, and we will bring you to the best.

Good to know

GREECE FACTS

Location

Greece sits at the southeastern tip of Europe, where the Balkan Peninsula meets the Aegean and Mediterranean Seas. Its terrain spans the mountainous mainland Peloponnese to over 200 inhabited islands, each offering its own distinct character and riding terrain.

Climate

Greece enjoys a classic Mediterranean climate, with hot, dry summers reaching 30°C+ across much of the mainland and islands. Spring and autumn offer particularly pleasant riding conditions, with milder temperatures and fewer crowds than peak summer.

Cuisine

Greek cuisine is built around fresh, simple ingredients — olive oil, feta, fresh seafood and vegetables — alongside classics like moussaka and souvlaki. Volcanic Santorini produces some of the country's most celebrated wines, particularly the crisp white Assyrtiko.

Terrain

From the mountainous roads of the Peloponnese connecting ancient historical sites to the dramatic White Mountains of Crete, Greece offers a genuinely varied mix of coastal and high-mountain terrain, much of it still quiet and undiscovered by cycling tourism.
