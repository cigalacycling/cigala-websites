The trip

LIVE THE GRAVEL DREAM

When you think about Tuscany, as a cycling lover you immediately picture the iconic white roads, rows of cypress trees, and those rolling green hills that seem to go on forever.

We've created a one-of-a-kind gravel adventure that takes you through the heart of the region—immersing you in authentic Tuscan culture, world-class food and wine, and breathtaking landscapes.

This isn’t just a cycling trip—it’s a journey through vineyards, medieval villages, and hidden trails you’d never find on your own.

With 80% off-road riding, you’ll experience the real Tuscany, far from the tourist paths and close to its soul. Whether you're chasing gravel dreams or just want to lose yourself in stunning scenery with like-minded riders, this is your chance.

Itinerary

DAY TO DAY BREAKDOWN

GPX files will be sent before the trip.

  1. DAY1

    ARRIVAL DAY

    After the airport pick up, we head straight to dinner in the town of Castagneto Carducci for our first Tuscan Meal.
    After dinner, setting up the bikes for the next day, when the gravel adventure begins!

  2. DAY2

    CASTAGNETO - PECCIOLI

    100 km1,200 m

    From the start, we are in Gravel, through forest by the Tyrrhenian Sea.
    After 15 km we go through the beautiful Bolgheri, where we find a famous stretch of Cypress trees for some nice pictures.
    Half way through the ride, we start climbing. We go through Bocelli's hometown Lajatico for then finishing in Peccioli.

  3. DAY3

    PECCIOLI - COLLE VAL D'ELSA

    70 km1,600 m

    On today's route we go by some of the most beautiful villages of Tuscany such as Volterra & San Gimignano for then finishing in Colle Val'Elsa.
    Up and down through medieval villages, and stunning countryside, where everywhere you look, is a postcard!

  4. DAY4

    COLLE VAL D'ELSA - PIENZA

    85 km1,600 m

    Possible the most beautiful day.
    Shortly after starting we go through the town of Monteriggioni, then in the famous Piazza Del Campo in Siena for lunch.
    After lunch, we continue heading south passing through section of the popular Strade Bianche for then soak in the most beautiful countryside that you can imagine when getting close to Pienza.

  5. DAY5

    PIENZA - MARINA DI GROSSETO

    120 km1,700 m
    The longest day on the saddle. Starting from Pienza and finishing by the sea in Marina di Grosseto.
    The first part of the ride, is up and down through medieval villages and countryside.
    The second part, as we get closer to the sea, the gravel changes so as the vegetation. Going through olives trees farms and natural reserves before reaching destination.
  6. DAY6

    MARINA DI GROSSETO - CASTAGNETO

    100 km1,100 m
    On the last day, you will experience something totally different from the previous days.
    The route start by the sea with incredible views of the cliffs and beaches. On the second part of the ride, we go more in-land going through forests, hills and farms to then finishing off where we started.
    After reaching destination, time for packing and enjoy our last Tuscan meal together.
  7. DAY7

    LEAVING DAY

    After breakfast, airport transfer to Pisa Airport.
    ​See you next time :-)
