The trip

RIDE THE TOUR DE FRANCE ICONIC CLIMBS: GALIBIER, ALPE D'HUEZ, MADELEINE, LA BONETTE & MORE

Conquer the legendary mountain passes of Tour de France history. From the dramatic switchbacks of Montvernier and the towering summit of Galibier to the famous 21 hairpins of Alpe d'Huez and the ultra-high altitude of Cime de la Bonette, this tour takes you across Europe's most celebrated cycling grounds.

Feel the pulse of cycling history beneath your wheels as you trace the roads carved into sheer granite and alpine meadows. Each day presents a fresh horizon of soaring peaks, crisp mountain air, and the intoxicating challenge of Europe’s iconic climbs. Beyond the tarmac, unwind in handpicked alpine villages, indulge in regional French gastronomy, and swap stories over a glass of local wine. With professional ride leaders guiding the way and full support van backing on every ascent, all you need to do is clip in, find your rhythm, and ride into legend.

Itinerary

DAY TO DAY BREAKDOWN

GPX files will be sent before the trip.

  1. DAY1

    ARRIVAL DAY & SHAKE DOWN RIDE

    Welcome to the French Alps!
    After checking in at the hotel, we get the bikes ready and if you are up for a short 1hr ride to test the bike, we will bring you up and down the valley. It’s the perfect, stress-free way to spin out the travel legs, fine-tune your setup, and get a first taste of the breathtaking mountain scenery before the big climbs begin. Afterwards, we’ll gather for a welcome drink and a brief tour orientation to run through the week ahead, introduce the support team, and get ready for an unforgettable week on the road.

    Hotel: Best Western Hôtel Cœur de Maurienne **** - Saint-Jean-de-Maurienne

  2. DAY2

    LACETS DE MONTVERNIER & COL DE LA MADELEINE

    90 km2,500 m

    A spectacular opening day in the Maurienne Valley.
    We warm up along the valley floor before taking on the Lacets de Montvernier—18 tight switchbacks stacked over just 3.4 km. After a quick descent, we tackle the main challenge: the iconic Col de la Madeleine (1,993 m), climbing 20 km through alpine forests to sweeping views of Mont Blanc before a long descent back to town.

    Hotel: Best Western Hôtel Cœur de Maurienne **** - Saint-Jean-de-Maurienne

  3. DAY3

    COL DU GLANDON & COL DE LA CROIX DE FER

    90 km2,900 m

    A classic double-pass Alpine loop. We head west to ascend the irregular, pitchy gradients of the Col du Glandon (1,924 m) before continuing directly up to the Col de la Croix de Fer (2,067 m). Marked by its famous iron cross, the summit offers stunning views of the Aiguilles d'Arves before a fast, technical descent back into the valley.

    Hotel: Best Western Hôtel Cœur de Maurienne **** - Saint-Jean-de-Maurienne

  4. DAY4

    COL DU TÉLÉGRAPHE & COL DU GALIBIER

    95 km2,700 m

    A true Alpine queen stage into high-altitude territory. We open with the steady climb of the Col du Télégraphe (1,563 m), followed by a brief descent to Valloire. Then comes the main event: the mighty Col du Galibier (2,642 m). Rise above the tree line into a wild landscape of rock and snow before a sweeping 20 km descent to Monêtier-les-Bains.

    Hotel: Maison Alliey Appart Hôtel & Spa **** Monêtier-les-Bains

  5. DAY5

    ALPE D'HUEZ

    35 km1,100 m

    Dedicated to cycling royalty. After a short van transfer to Bourg-d'Oisans, we tackle the 21 hairpins of Alpe d'Huez (1,850 m). Every meter of this 13.8 km ascent is steeped in Tour de France history. Enjoy a victory coffee at the summit before descending back down to the valley for a relaxing afternoon at the hotel spa.

    Hotel: Maison Alliey Appart Hôtel & Spa **** Monêtier-les-Bains

  6. DAY6

    COL D'IZOARD & COL DE VARS

    115 km2,700 m

    A magnificent point-to-point journey through the Southern Alps. Passing Briançon, we ascend the dramatic Col d'Izoard (2,360 m) before descending toward Guillestre. We then tackle our second pass of the day, the legendary Col de Vars (2,108 m), followed by a long, picturesque descent that carries us straight into Barcelonnette.

    Accommodation: Hôtel Azteca & Spa **** - Barcelonnette

  7. DAY7

    COL DE LA BONNETTE

    60 km1,800 m

    The grand finale takes us to the roof of the tour. Departing Jausiers, we climb the steady slopes of the Col de la Bonette up to the Cime de la Bonette (2,802 m)—one of the highest paved roads in Europe. Soak in 360-degree Alpine views before an epic 24 km descent back to the valley to celebrate.

    Accommodation: Hôtel Azteca & Spa **** - Barcelonnette

  8. DAY8

    LEAVING DAY

    After breakfast, airport transfer to Nice Airport. ​See you next time 🙂

Good to know

Frequently Asked Questions

Do I need to bring my own bike, or can I rent one?

You're welcome to bring your own bike, or rent one for the week. After booking, you will receive a form to fill out via email. You can let us know there and in the case you want to rent, you will see an option to fill up details for your measurements. 
You can choose a range of bikes: road or e-road bike.

What fitness level do I need for this trip?

This trip suits Intermediate and Expert riders. Each days have lots of climbing to complete, therefore, a good level of fitness is required to participate to this tour. If unsure, you can always opt for a road e-bike.

What kind of support is included on this trip?

A daily support van follows the group throughout the trip, giving you the full pro experience. It carries spares, snacks, drinks, and any spare gear or jackets you want to shed mid-ride.
Additionally the van will move your suitcases from one hotel to the other.
Finally, our guides are expert mechanics, so you can rely for any adjustments, punctures etc.

Is there a single room supplement?

Yes, a single room supplement is available and you choose it during the booking process.

If I bring my bike, what would happen to the bike box since we change hotel each day?

Your bike box will need to be transferred each day. We therefore recommend a compact bike box or even better for a stress free experience to rent a bike.  

What would be the weather?

It's summer, so expect warm days on the bike. At the top of mountain passes you can expect cooler temperatures. 

Should I specifically train for a trip like this?

If you're planning to be a serious and enjoying the climbs and arrive in the evening a little fresher a bit of training preparation is recommended. If you'd like a structured plan built around this trip, visit our cycling coaching page and Matteo can help you put together a plan based on your current level and the time you have before you travel.

What should I pack for this trip?

Beyond your usual riding kit, bring extra layers, and some rain gear just in case you meet the odd shower. Bring a jacket for descents, as some descents are quite long. Our full guide to packing for a European cycling holiday covers everything else you'll need for the week.

Cycling Alpe d'Huez: Complete Guide

No climb in cycling has quite the same cultural weight as Alpe d'Huez. The 21 numbered hairpins, each bearing the name of a Tour de France stage winner who triumphed there, have turned this from a simple mountain road into something closer to a pilgrimage site for cyclists. I've ridden it more times than I can count, and the atmosphere on race weekend — thousands of fans lining every hairpin — is genuinely unlike anywhere else in cycling.
