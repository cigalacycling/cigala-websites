Lake Garda

NO BETTER PLACE FOR CYCLING

There is no better place than Lake Garda for cycling lovers. The breathtaking scenery and unparalleled natural beauty guarantee all enthusiasts an unforgettable time of complete and utter freedom. The tours are based at this stunning lake, a common training spot for professional cycling teams in Italy. Here you can cycle in amazing scenery and routes, have the best italian food and unique experiences. Our aim is to help make your holiday special, by providing the best bike rides and the best activities. For Triathlete it's a great place to run. For swimming there is the lake and as mentioned amazing routes for cycling.

Lake Garda or Benaco is the largest and the cleanest lake in Italy. Its wider part to the south is surrounded by morainic hills that were left following the glacier recession, while the narrow northern part is enclosed by the mountains that create its fjord-shape configuration and provide a very mild Mediterranean climate.

Lake Garda is a unique destination and getting around to discover the culture and local traditions is easy at any time of the year. The luminous environment, temperate climate, and flourishing vegetation - mainly olive and lemon groves, palms, cypresses, oleanders and orange trees - along with the majestic landscapes are the background to the remarkable historical and cultural remains spread all around and make this lake the most charming in Italy. Given the intense colour of its water with a marked shade of blue and matchless transparency, Lake Garda is a unique spot where visitors and guests can enjoy a holiday full of relaxation, sports, fun and the thrill of discovering new places

When you think of ‘Italy’ you immediately think of ‘food’, the two words are synonymous with each other. The Italian culture and way of life revolves almost entirely around food and although based on simple recipes, Italian food is very diverse. The ‘cucina’ of the region of Lake Garda is with it’s ‘Brescian’, ‘Veronese’ and mediterranean influences, and local specialities from the mountains or the lake, is among the best. There are literally thousands of restaurants in Lake Garda, set in and wonderful locations in the beautiful towns around the lake, and we will bring you in the best.

Good to know

LAKE GARDA FACTS

Location

Italy's biggest lake, with over 100 miles of shoreline that traverse three regions of Italy, Lombardy, Trentino-Alto adige and Veneto, Lake Garda has a hugely diverse landscape, culture, history and traditions.

Climate

Lake Garda has a sub-Mediterranean climate, so mild and different from the other lakes that it can be defined as the “Benaco climate”.

The average winter temperature varies between 12° and 18°C and in summer between 24° and 30°C.

Cuisine

The Italian Cuisine in the Lake Garda area is something you'll want to know about.

The area has some popular dishes that are known all over Italy.

Among the usual pizzas and pastas, some other popular products are extra virgin olive oils, white and red wine as well as Limoncino liqueur

Terrain

With its vineyards nestled high in the surrounding verdant mountains, lemon and olive trees lining the streets and chocolate box harbour towns perched delicately on the lakefront, Lago di Garda is a cultural culmination of the Italian peninsula.

Windy roads, rolling terrain with a huge selection of short and long climbs.
