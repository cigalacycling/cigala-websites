Switzerland

THE ULTIMATE ALPINE ADVENTURE

There is no better place than Switzerland for cyclists who dream of the highest, most legendary mountain passes in Europe. Precision-engineered roads, immaculate alpine scenery and some of the most storied climbs in cycling history guarantee every rider a sense of complete and utter freedom. Our tours are based around some of the country's most iconic terrain, including passes that have shaped the history of the Tour de Suisse and the Giro d'Italia. Here you can climb roads used by cycling's greatest names, enjoy exceptional Alpine cuisine, and experience mountain scenery that few destinations in the world can match. Our aim is to help make your holiday special, by providing the best routes and the best experiences.

Our tours are based around some of the country's most spectacular terrain, a training ground long favoured by professional cycling teams for its quiet, well-surfaced roads. Here you can cycle through dramatic mountain passes, taste some of Central Europe's best food and wine, and experience a destination most cyclists have never had on their radar.

Switzerland's cycling appeal comes from an unmatched concentration of high-mountain passes within a relatively compact country. The Gotthard, Furka, Grimsel and Susten passes sit within striking distance of one another, connected by immaculately maintained roads and some of the most dramatic alpine scenery anywhere on the continent.

Switzerland sits at the heart of the Alps, and its cycling culture reflects a national obsession with precision, quality and the mountains themselves. Beyond the legendary passes, the country's lakes — Geneva, Lucerne, Lugano — offer stunning, gentler riding for recovery days between the big climbing efforts. Given its combination of world-class passes and immaculate infrastructure, Switzerland is a destination where visitors can enjoy a holiday full of serious climbing, striking scenery and the thrill of riding some of the most famous roads in cycling.

When you think of Swiss food, expect hearty, mountain-shaped cuisine built for refuelling after a big day of climbing. Cheese fondue, raclette and rösti are the country's signature dishes, alongside excellent alpine cheeses and, of course, some of the world's finest chocolate. Switzerland's varied regions — German, French and Italian-speaking — each bring their own culinary traditions, giving genuine variety across a single trip. There are countless mountain huts and restaurants throughout the country, set in spectacular alpine locations, and we will bring you to the best.

Good to know

SWITZERLAND FACTS

Location

Switzerland sits at the heart of the Alps in Central Europe, bordering Italy, France, Germany and Austria. Its terrain is dominated by some of the highest, most celebrated mountain passes in cycling, connected by an exceptionally well-maintained road network.

Climate

Switzerland has an alpine climate that varies significantly with altitude — warm, pleasant summers in the valleys around 22-25°C, with much cooler conditions at altitude on the high passes, some of which remain snow-covered well into early summer.

Cuisine

Swiss cuisine reflects the country's mountain geography and multilingual regions. Expect cheese fondue, raclette, rösti and exceptional alpine cheeses, alongside some of the world's finest chocolate — hearty, high-quality food built for refuelling after serious climbing.

Terrain

Switzerland is home to some of the most legendary mountain passes in cycling, including the Gotthard, Furka, Grimsel and Susten. Roads are immaculately maintained and well-graded even at extreme altitude, offering some of the purest high-mountain climbing anywhere in Europe.
