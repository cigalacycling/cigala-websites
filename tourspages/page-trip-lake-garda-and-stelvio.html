The trip

LAKE GARDA, ITALY'S HIDDEN PARADISE

Yes, a paradise.
There is no better place than Lake Garda for cycling lovers. The breathtaking scenery and unparalleled natural beauty guarantee all enthusiasts an unforgettable time of complete and utter freedom

During this trip, you will experience cycling in one of Europe’s top destinations while fully immersing yourself in authentic Italian culture and cuisine.

Every day is unique around the lake — with diverse routes that take you through olive groves, vineyards, and picturesque towns framed by sparkling waters and majestic mountains. The mild climate and well-maintained roads make it ideal for riders of all levels.

What truly makes this trip special is the optional day trip to Passo dello Stelvio — Italy’s greatest climb and one of the most iconic cycling ascents in the world.

This Lake Garda cycling experience is sure to exceed your expectations. It’s the perfect blend of adventure, culture, and natural beauty — a cycling holiday you’ll remember forever.

Itinerary

DAY TO DAY BREAKDOWN

GPX files will be sent before the trip.

  1. DAY1

    ARRIVAL DAY AND SIRMIONE

    35 km | 250 m After arrival and after having some lunch, when everybody is ready and have finished adjusting the bikes, we head out for a 35km spin to Sirmione peninsula.
  2. DAY2

    TREMOSINE - STRADA DELLA FORRA

    100 km | 1,300 m Today we head to the west side of the lake where we climb the most beautiful climb of the lake towards Tremosine. On these roads, the James Bond movie was shot. At the top, we have some lunch in the beautiful terrace restaurant. After that, we head back.
  3. DAY3

    PASSO DELLO STELVIO OR OPTIONAL DAY

    45 km | 1,530 m 

    Today if there is an optional day-trip to Italy's greatest climb: Passo Dello Stelvio.
    We climb from the most popular side, Prato.
    It is a 3.5 hrs drive out and then 3.5 hrs back. 25km of climb, 48 switchbacks to 2757mt of altitude.

    Those not fancy Stelvio, would have another optional route in Lake Garda.

  4. DAY4

    THE INLAND

    85 km | 300 m On today's ride we head to the inland of Lake Garda and we cover some beautiful and quiet roads.
  5. DAY5

    LOOP OF THE LAKE

    145 km | 500 m The longest day in terms of distance. 145km around the Lake clockwise. An incredible route, mostly flat, with unparalleled scenery.
  6. DAY6

    COFFEE RIDE

    50 km | 120 m

    On the last day, before catching the flight back, we head on the west side of the Lake for a short ride and get a nice coffee/lunch on the waterfront.

Frequently Asked Questions

Do I need to bring my own bike, or can I rent one?

You're welcome to bring your own bike, or rent one for the week. After booking, you will receive a form to fill out via email. You can let us know there and in the case you want to rent, you will see an option to fill up details for your measurements. 

What fitness level do I need for this trip?

This trip suits Beginner, Intermediate and Expert riders. The core Lake Garda routes are approachable for most fitness levels — the optional Stelvio day is the only day that demands solid climbing fitness, however is optional.

Are support vehicles included?

On the Lake Garda tour, we offer the support van on the loop of the lake day. Of course, in the Stelvio day, the van will carry your descending gear and jacket and will be at the top. 

Is there a single room supplement?

Yes, a single room supplement applies if you want a private room in a shared two bedroom apartment. There is also the option to have your own apartment.

Do I have to ride the full Stelvio climb, or can I skip that day?

The Stelvio day trip is a highlight for many guests, but it's not compulsory — if you'd rather have a rest day or ride a shorter route around Lake Garda instead, just let your guide know.

I see you have a gravel tour running at the same time, can I join one or two gravel rides?

Yes our Lake Garda Gravel will be running at the same time. And of course, you can join also join any of the gravel rides, just let your guide during the trip. If you need to rent a gravel bike for the day, we can organise it for you. Just let us know when booking in the case you need to rent.

Should I specifically train for a trip like this?

If you're planning to ride the Stelvio day, some dedicated climbing preparation makes a real difference — sustained gradients like this reward riders who've built specific climbing endurance rather than just general fitness. If you'd like a structured plan built around this trip, visit our cycling coaching page and Matteo can help you put together a plan based on your current level and the time you have before you travel.

What should I pack for this trip?

 Beyond your usual riding kit, bring layers for the Stelvio day — temperatures at the summit can be significantly cooler than down at lake level, even in summer. Our full guide to packing for a European cycling holiday covers everything else you'll need for the week.
