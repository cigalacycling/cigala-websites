The trip

RIDE INTO THE LEGEND , TAKE PART IN THE GRAN FONDO STRADE BIANCHE

Every cyclist has heard of the iconic Strade Bianche—that thrilling, dust-filled race across the historic white gravel roads of Tuscany. It's a modern classic, blending professional racing with the soul of cycling’s golden era.

But what if you could experience it yourself—not from the sidelines, but from the saddle? We've created a unique cycling holiday based in the heart of the Chianti region, designed specifically for riders who want to immerse themselves in the magic of Tuscany, challenge themselves on the same gravel sectors as the pros, and ride in one of the most atmospheric amateur events in Europe: the Gran Fondo Strade Bianche.

The events have two routes for you to choose from: the 'Gran Fondo' and the 'Medio Fondo'.
The 'Gran Fondo' is 137km with 31.4km of white roads.
The 'Medio Fondo' is 87km with 26.6km of white roads.
The start and finish of both routes is the same, with the finish in Piazza Del Campo, the main square of Siena.

Your race entry is secured through the OFFICIAL TOUR OPERATOR of Gran Fondo Strade Bianche.

This mean that by booking this trip you will have GUARANTEED ENTRY to the event.


However, please note that we are now SOLD OUT on Gran Fondo (137km) Entries. By Booking we can only guarantee Medio Fondo (87km) Entries.

Itinerary

DAY TO DAY BREAKDOWN

GPX files will be sent before the trip.

  1. DAY1

    ARRIVAL DAY AND SHAKE DOWN RIDE

    30 km700 m

    Welcome to Tuscany.
    After arriving at the hotel and setting up the bikes, we head for a short shake down ride to Gaiole in Chianti. Home of the Eroica. A stunning rolling route through the Chianti hills with one section of white roads.

  2. DAY2

    WHITE ROADS AND STRADE BIANCHE RECEE

    80 km1,500 m

    Today we get a chance to test out the legs and route of Gran Fondo Strade Bianche.
    We head south towards Siena, we cover the sectors of Monteaperti, Colle Pinzuto, Tolfe and the last climb into town Santa Caterina. After lunch in Piazza del Campo, we head back to the hotel. You can grab a massage or just chill at the indoor pool before dinner. 

  3. DAY3

    CHIANTI LOOP

    57 km1,000 m

    The morning is dedicated to riding through the heart of Chianti, a rolling route that strings together some of the best roads in the area. Expect cypress-lined climbs, stretches of classic strade bianche underwheel, and vineyards stretching out in every direction.
    Once we're back at the hotel, the rest of the day is yours. Spend the afternoon relaxing by the indoor pool, book a massage, or join a guided wine tasting at Castello di Albola, one of the standout wineries in the region.

  4. DAY4

    CASTELLINA LOOP AND COFFEE RIDE

    37 km700 m

    Today's a lighter day — an easy coffee ride to Castellina in Chianti to shake out the legs before tomorrow. We head to Castellina and we stop for coffee along the way, soaking in the vineyards and cypress trees.
    Back at the hotel, we head to Siena to watch the pro race — women and men — live. There's something special about standing trackside and watching the world's best battle over the very strade bianche we've been riding all week.

  5. DAY5

    GRAN FONDO STRADE BIANCHE

    144 km2,000 m

    Today is the big one — the Gran Fondo Strade Bianche, the day this whole trip has been building toward. We take on the same iconic white roads as the pros, weaving through the hills south of Siena on a route packed with cypress-lined climbs and the crunch of gravel under our tyres. It's tough, it's dusty, and it's unforgettable — a true bucket-list ride for anyone who loves this sport.
    We finish back in the heart of Siena, rolling into Piazza del Campo just like the professionals do, with a well-earned sense of achievement and plenty of stories to tell over dinner.

  6. DAY6

    LEAVING DAY

    After an unforgettable week of riding, exploring, and experiencing the magic of Tuscany, it’s time to say arrivederci.

    Whether you’re heading home or continuing your travels, we hope you leave with tired legs, great memories, and a deeper love for cycling.

    Grazie mille for being part of the journey—and we hope to ride with you again soon!

Frequently Asked Questions

Do I need to bring my own bike, or can I rent one?

You're welcome to bring your own bike, or rent one for the week. After booking, you will receive a form to fill out via email. You can let us know there and in the case you want to rent, you will see an option to fill up details for your measurements. 
You can choose a range of bikes: road, gravel or e-road bike.

What fitness level do I need for this trip?

This trip suits Beginner, Intermediate and Expert riders. The core Strade Bianche routes are approachable for most fitness levels — on Gran Fondo day you have the option to choose a longer or a shorter route based on your fitness level.

What kind of support is included on this trip?

A daily support van follows the group throughout the trip, giving you the full pro experience. It carries spares, snacks, drinks, and any spare gear or jackets you want to shed mid-ride.

During the Gran Fondo Strade Bianche, we set up our own dedicated feed zones along the route — just like the pros get. You'll find snacks and drinks stocked and ready, and you can drop off your own bidons or specific nutrition ahead of time so it's waiting for you when you ride through. Our team is on hand at each zone, and there's basic mechanical assistance available too, just in case you need a quick fix to keep going.

Is there a single room supplement?

Yes, a single room supplement is available and you choose it during the booking process.

Is best a road or a gravel bike for this tour?

A road bike with 30-32mm tires is certainly more than enough. You can also do it with 28mm. A gravel bike would be a little slower but more comfortable.

Once I book the trip is the entry to the Gran Fondo guaranteed?

Yes we purchase tickets in advanced and once you book the trip your entry to the Gran Fondo is Guaranteed. 
After booking, you will receive an email with the grid options available for you to choose from. Grid 1 and 2 are only available to tour operators, meaning that with us you can start near the front.

Should I specifically train for a trip like this?

If you're planning to ride the Gran Fondo, some endurance and climbing preparation makes a real difference — steep gradients on white roads like this reward riders who've built specific climbing endurance rather than just general fitness. If you'd like a structured plan built around this trip, visit our cycling coaching page and Matteo can help you put together a plan based on your current level and the time you have before you travel.

What should I pack for this trip?

Beyond your usual riding kit, bring extra layers and some rain gear since is early season and you can expect the occasional shower. Our full guide to packing for a European cycling holiday covers everything else you'll need for the week.
