The trip

A CYCLIST'S DREAM

On this trip, you’ll ride through the Dolomites — one of the most spectacular landscapes on Earth.

Towering limestone peaks, alpine meadows, and dramatic passes create a truly unique setting.

A UNESCO World Heritage Site and a bucket-list destination for cyclists, every turn feels like a postcard — where beauty meets challenge.

You’ll ride some of Italy’s most iconic climbs, each with its own character and story: Passo Giau, the Sella Ronda (Gardena, Sella, Pordoi, Campolongo), Falzarego and Valparola, Fedaia beneath the Marmolada glacier, and the legendary Tre Cime di Lavaredo.

This is more than a cycling tour — it’s a journey through the heart of the Dolomites, where every climb brings you closer to nature, culture, and your limits.

Itinerary

DAY TO DAY BREAKDOWN

GPX files will be sent before the trip.

  1. DAY1

    ARRIVAL DAY AND TRANSFER TO LA VILLA

    After arrival, enjoy the drive to La Villa and spend the afternoon in the beautiful town.
    Adjust and test your bike before settle in for a beautiful meal overlooking the dolomites.

  2. DAY2

    SELLA RONDA WITH A TWIST

    80 km2,400 m

    On today’s menu: one of the most iconic and breathtaking routes in the Dolomites — the Sella Ronda, with a twist. We add an extra challenge to the classic loop by starting with the Passo Valparola, setting the tone for an unforgettable day in the mountains. From there, we dive into the legendary circuit, tackling a sequence of world-renowned climbs: Passo Pordoi, with its smooth switchbacks and sweeping views; Passo Sella, dramatic and majestic beneath the jagged peaks; and Passo Gardena, offering a perfect blend of beauty and flow. This loop is not just a ride — it’s a showcase of the very best the Dolomites have to offer: iconic climbs, surreal alpine landscapes, and a sense of wonder around every corner. A true highlight of the week.

  3. DAY3

    GIAU DAY

    80 km2,400 m

    Today’s epic loop by taking you first over the iconic Passo Campolongo, a classic Dolomites climb with stunning views and smooth gradients.

    Next, you tackle the Passo Giau, one of the most spectacular and challenging climbs in the Dolomites, famous for its steep sections and jaw-dropping panoramas. After conquering Giau, you descend toward Passo Falzarego, a climb rich in history and surrounded by dramatic rock formations. The route continues over Passo Valparola, offering sweeping alpine vistas before descending back to La Villa. This loop combines some of the Dolomites’ most iconic passes, delivering a day full of challenge, beauty, and unforgettable riding.

  4. DAY4

    TRE CIME DI LAVAREDO & TRANSFER TO CANAZEI

    50 km1,700 m

    After checking out of the hotel, we transfer by van to the beautiful town of Cortina d’Ampezzo, where today’s ride begins. We start by climbing the scenic Passo Tre Croci, a steady ascent that warms up the legs while offering stunning views of the surrounding peaks. At the summit, we enjoy a few kilometers of flat riding along the crystal-clear waters of Lake Misurina, a truly magical spot nestled beneath towering mountains. From here, the iconic climb to Tre Cime di Lavaredo begins — one of the most breathtaking and unforgettable ascents you will ever experience, with dramatic rock formations and panoramic vistas at every turn. After conquering Tre Cime, we descend back to Cortina, where the bikes are loaded into the van for a short transfer to Canazei, your home for the next two nights. Check into your new hotel, relax, and get ready for more epic riding in the days ahead.

  5. DAY5

    SAN PELLEGRINO & FEDAIA

    88 km2,200 m

    Today we explore a new valley (Val Di Fassa), opening up a fresh set of iconic climbs. We’ll ride the challenging Passo San Pellegrino, a favorite among cyclists for its smooth gradients and beautiful alpine scenery. Next, we tackle Passo Fedaia, where the views reach another level — at the summit, you’ll be greeted by the stunning Marmolada glacier and the shimmering lake below, a truly breathtaking sight.

    From there, enjoy a thrilling descent down into Canazei, marking the final ride of the trip with spectacular landscapes and a well-earned sense of accomplishment.

  6. DAY6

    LEAVING DAY

    25 km100 m

    After breakfast, a quick coffee ride in the valley.
    After that we check out from the hotel, grab some lunch and we head to the airport for your return flight. See you next time! :-)
