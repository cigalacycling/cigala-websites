Our mission

All of your cycling needs at your fingertips

Since its founding in 2016, Cigala Cycling’s mission is to bring all of the cyclist’s needs in one place and make them easily available at your fingertips.

Cigala Cycling was founded by Matteo Cigala when he moved from Italy to Ireland. A former pro cyclist with 28 years in the sport, he combines sports science, racing experience and close links with professional teams to help riders of every level train smarter and ride faster.

  • European Road Champion Gran Fondo
  • UCI Gran Fondo Vice World Champion
  • UCI Gravel Vice World Champion
  • Italian Gravel Champion
  • 200+ race wins
